Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Rajesh Sharma, a diligent District Magistrate of Ramgarh, has earned a reputation for his integrity and swift action against illegal mining and environmental violations. His district is ecologically sensitive, and he has recently spearheaded a crackdown on unauthorized sand extraction along the riverbeds, a major revenue source for local contractors but a severe ecological threat.

During a recent raid, one of the confiscated trucks belonged to a transport company owned by his younger brother-in-law, who operates several such vehicles across the district. Rajesh's brother-in-law approaches him, pleading for a lenient approach, emphasizing potential financial ruin and family distress. Simultaneously, a local environmental activist group demands strict enforcement, and a senior political leader from the ruling party subtly advises a "pragmatic" resolution to avoid embarrassing local business interests.

The decision will directly impact his family's financial stability, his professional reputation, the environmental protection efforts, and public trust in the administration. Both strict action and undue leniency carry significant ethical and practical consequences for all involved.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
The case involves conflict of interest, integrity versus personal relations, public trust, rule of law versus political/familial pressure, and environmental protection versus economic distress. Upholding impartiality and accountability are key ethical dilemmas.

(b) What are the options available to Rajesh Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and merits. (100 words)
1. Proceed strictly as per law: Merits - Upholds rule of law, maintains integrity, protects environment, strengthens public trust. Demerits - Strains family relations, political backlash, potential financial ruin for brother-in-law.
2. Grant leniency to brother-in-law: Merits - Protects family, eases political pressure. Demerits - Compromises integrity, violates rule of law, damages public trust, encourages corruption, harms environment.
3. Recuse himself from the case: Merits - Avoids direct conflict of interest, maintains personal integrity. Demerits - May be seen as dereliction of duty, might not fully remove pressure, potential for influence on successor.

(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
I would recommend Rajesh Sharma proceed strictly as per law without any leniency. This upholds the fundamental principles of public service: integrity, impartiality, and the rule of law. Any deviation would establish a dangerous precedent, erode public trust, and compromise environmental protection.

To handle pressures, Rajesh should officially recuse himself from direct involvement in his brother-in-law's specific case, ensuring due process is followed by another competent officer or committee under his overall supervision. He must clearly communicate his decision to his brother-in-law, explaining the paramount importance of his official duty. To the political leader, he should firmly state his commitment to constitutional principles and uniform application of law, citing the need for transparent governance. He should proactively engage with the environmental group, assuring them of impartial action. Documentation of all communication and decisions will be crucial.
