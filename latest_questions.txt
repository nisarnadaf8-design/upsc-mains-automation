Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Alok Sharma, a highly respected and diligent District Collector of Ramanagara District, Karnataka, is known for his unwavering integrity and commitment to fair administration. He has recently initiated a focused drive to resolve a backlog of long-pending land dispute cases, which are a major source of litigation and public grievance in his district, often involving complex ancestral claims and local power dynamics.

One such critical land dispute case, concerning a significant agricultural plot with considerable market value, has now reached Alok's desk for final adjudication after successfully navigating initial levels of inquiry and local recommendations. To his dismay, Alok discovers that his younger brother, Raghav Sharma, is one of the primary parties claiming ownership of the land. Raghav, aware of his brother's influential position, has already approached Alok privately, urging him to "look favorably" at his claim, while a local political strongman, who subtly supports the opposing party, has indicated that "justice must be seen to be done without any familial bias."

Alok's decision will directly impact his brother's financial future, the livelihood of the opposing claimant who is a small farmer, the public's trust in the district administration, and Alok's own professional reputation built over years. Both taking a decision himself in this situation or recusing without proper justification carry significant repercussions for ethical governance and the perception of justice.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Alok Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
