Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Baldev Singh, an experienced Joint Secretary in the Ministry of Infrastructure Development, New Delhi, is overseeing a critical national highway expansion project. This project involves extensive land acquisition and significant public-private partnerships, vital for national connectivity and economic growth.

An RTI applicant, a prominent civil society activist, has sought comprehensive financial records, internal feasibility studies, and detailed vendor negotiation minutes related to the project's land acquisition phase. While releasing all information would uphold transparency, Baldev's superior, the Secretary, has strongly advised against full disclosure, citing potential compromises to national strategic interests due to the project's sensitive locations and ongoing confidential commercial negotiations that could impact future public investments. Baldev also faces pressure from local media highlighting alleged irregularities, demanding greater accountability.

The decision will affect the public's right to information, the integrity of government processes, the financial viability of future strategic projects, and Baldev's professional reputation. Both full disclosure and complete denial carry significant ethical, economic, and political consequences.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Baldev Singh? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
