Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Arjun Sharma, an experienced IAS officer of the 2015 batch, is currently serving as the District Magistrate of Sagar district, known for its flood-prone regions. In July 2026, severe monsoon floods devastated several blocks, displacing thousands and causing extensive damage. The state government swiftly released emergency relief funds amounting to INR 50 Crores, placing Arjun in charge of their immediate disbursement for essential supplies, temporary shelters, and rehabilitation.

Just as the relief operations gained momentum, Arjun received a direct call from a powerful state minister, Mr. Rakesh Verma, who subtly but firmly suggested diverting a significant portion of the funds (around 10-15%) towards a 'development project' in his constituency, which was not flood-affected. Simultaneously, Arjun's close cousin, who runs a construction business, approached him, offering a substantial 'kickback' if he could secure contracts for temporary shelter construction, bypassing standard procurement procedures. Public expectations for swift and transparent relief are extremely high, with media closely scrutinizing every move.

Arjun's decision will directly affect the flood victims who desperately need aid, the integrity of the administration, and public trust. Complying could alleviate immediate political pressure but would leave thousands suffering and compromise ethical governance, while refusal risks his career and personal safety.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Arjun Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
