Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Riya Sharma, a conscientious District Magistrate in Aridpur District, a rapidly industrializing region, has recently taken charge with a mandate to ensure effective governance and social welfare. During a surprise inspection of industrial units in the district, aimed at assessing labour compliance and safety standards, she personally entered a manufacturing facility.

During her inspection, she discovered several children, visibly underage, working in hazardous conditions within the premises of "Agni Textiles." Investigations confirmed they were engaged in child labour. Further inquiry revealed that "Agni Textiles" is owned by Mr. Suresh Kumar, a powerful cabinet minister in the state government. Immediately after the discovery, Riya received an indirect but stern call from the Minister's office, suggesting that such "minor issues" be handled with "discretion" to avoid "unnecessary political noise." Her Chief Secretary also advised "extreme caution" given the Minister's political clout, hinting at potential career repercussions.

Riya's decision will directly impact the future of the exploited children, the Minister's political standing, the credibility of the district administration, and her own professional integrity. Both swift action and cautious inaction carry significant and far-reaching consequences for all involved stakeholders.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Riya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
