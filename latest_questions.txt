Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ms. Ananya Sharma is the District Magistrate (DM) of Rudraprayag, a district with a sensitive social fabric. Recently, a minor local dispute was amplified on social media, leading to communal tension and isolated incidents of violence. The district is now on edge, requiring careful administrative handling.

In the midst of this volatile situation, Ms. Sharma receives an informal directive from the Chief Minister’s office. She is instructed to take a visibly stern and punitive action, predominantly targeting one community, as this approach is believed to appease a powerful political constituency and secure "stability." Her immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, has also subtly advised compliance, hinting at potential adverse career repercussions if she defies the political will. However, confidential intelligence reports suggest that any biased action would only inflame passions, leading to widespread communal violence and significant loss of life and property.

The communities involved, the local police force, the credibility of the district administration, and the overarching principle of the rule of law are all stakeholders in Ms. Sharma’s decision. Both acceding to the political pressure and upholding her ethical duties carry severe and potentially far-reaching consequences.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
