Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Smita Sharma, a young and upright District Magistrate in the industrially developing district of Rampur, has initiated a rigorous drive to clear illegal encroachments on government land earmarked for a public park and community center. The project is crucial for the city's green spaces and social infrastructure, and she is known for her strict adherence to rules.

A powerful state minister, Mr. Rakesh Verma, who has significant political influence in the region, personally calls Smita. He insists that a particular encroachment, owned by a close associate of his who is also a major party funder, be exempted from the demolition drive, citing 'humanitarian grounds' and 'minor deviation'. Her immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, subtly advises her to 'handle the situation pragmatically' to avoid unnecessary political friction, hinting at potential career repercussions.

Smita's decision will directly affect the public's trust in governance, the environmental future of the city, and the principle of rule of law. Bending the rules could lead to further encroachments and loss of public land, while upholding them could jeopardize her career and invite political backlash.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Smita Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
