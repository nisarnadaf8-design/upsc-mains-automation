GS-1 SOCIETY: Globalisation and Its Impact on Indian Society
========================================

Q1. (10 Marks | 150 words)
Examine how the pervasive influence of digital platforms, a key facet of globalisation, has simultaneously fostered cultural convergence and amplified regional distinctiveness within Indian society. Discuss the implications of this dual phenomenon for social cohesion.

Q2. (10 Marks | 150 words)
How has the 'platformisation' of work, accelerated by globalisation, reshaped traditional employer-employee relationships and consequently impacted social security frameworks for a significant segment of the Indian workforce? Critically evaluate the emergent challenges.

Q3. (15 Marks | 250 words)
Critically analyse the multi-faceted impact of globalisation on women's socio-economic status in India, considering aspects of skill development, labour market participation, challenges to traditional gender roles, and the rise of new vulnerabilities within the digital economy. Illustrate with relevant examples.

Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Aarav Sharma, a dedicated District Development Officer (DDO) in Sagar district, is overseeing the implementation of the 'Gramin Ujjwal Yojana', a flagship government welfare scheme aimed at improving rural infrastructure and livelihoods. The scheme's progress is critical for the district's development targets, and a comprehensive annual report on its impact and expenditure is due to be submitted to the state government within the next two weeks.

During the preparation of the report, Aarav discovers significant discrepancies between the ground reality and the official figures submitted by some Block Development Officers (BDOs) under him. The data suggests that beneficiary reach and infrastructural development targets have been underachieved by a considerable margin. His immediate superior, the District Collector, along with a prominent local political leader, strongly advises Aarav to 'adjust' the figures to reflect a more favourable outcome, emphasizing the political sensitivity and potential backlash if the actual numbers are revealed.

Aarav is now faced with a stark choice: present an accurate but unflattering report, or comply with the pressure to manipulate data. His decision will directly impact the allocation of future funds for genuine beneficiaries, the credibility of the government scheme, public trust in administration, and potentially his own career progression.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Aarav Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
