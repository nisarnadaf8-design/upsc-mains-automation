Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Ms. Anjali Singh, a diligent District Welfare Officer in Ayodhya District, Uttar Pradesh, is overseeing the implementation of the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin) for the current financial year. Her department is tasked with preparing a comprehensive report on the scheme's progress, detailing the number of completed housing units and beneficiaries, which is crucial for further fund disbursements and annual performance reviews.

As the deadline for the report submission approaches, Ms. Singh is subjected to intense pressure from Mr. Rajiv Shukla, the influential local MLA, and subtly, from her immediate superior, the District Magistrate, Mr. Alok Verma. Both indicate the necessity to present a significantly higher completion rate and beneficiary coverage than the actual ground reality, citing "political sensitivities" and the need to showcase "exemplary progress" ahead of upcoming state elections. Anjali knows this would involve manipulating data, as many units are either incomplete or incorrectly tagged. A junior officer, frustrated by similar pressures, has discreetly warned her that such falsification would undermine public trust and might lead to future scrutiny.

Ms. Singh's decision will directly impact the legitimate beneficiaries awaiting housing, the credibility of the government scheme, public faith in administration, and her own professional integrity and career. Compliance risks a cover-up and systemic fraud, while defiance could lead to professional repercussions and strained working relationships within the district administration.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Anjali Singh? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
