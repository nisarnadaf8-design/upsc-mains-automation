Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Paragraph 1 — Background and Context:
Ms. Anya Sharma, a diligent Deputy Secretary in the Ministry of Finance, was posted in the Internal Audit Department of a large public sector undertaking (PSU) involved in infrastructure development in a tier-2 city. Her primary mandate was to ensure financial propriety and transparency in major project expenditures, a role crucial for upholding public trust and efficient resource utilization.

Paragraph 2 — The Dilemma:
During a routine audit, Anya uncovered significant financial irregularities and potential fraud amounting to several crores in a flagship highway construction project, directly implicating a senior director within the PSU and hinting at the involvement of a high-ranking political functionary. Despite initial hesitation due to the sensitivity of the matter, she formally reported these findings through established whistleblower protection channels within the ministry. Subsequently, she faced immediate and intense retaliation: her official reports were mysteriously suppressed, she received an abrupt transfer order to a non-existent liaison post in a remote area, and subtle threats concerning her family's safety and reputation began circulating through unofficial channels. Her immediate superior, citing "organizational peace" and the need to avoid "unnecessary political interference," strongly advised her to retract her allegations.

Paragraph 3 — Stakeholders and Consequences:
Her decision will significantly impact the financial health of the PSU, public trust in governance, the careers of the accused individuals, and the morale of other honest officers. Inaction would compromise her integrity and perpetuate corruption, while persistence could jeopardize her career, personal safety, and family's well-being.

Sub-questions:
(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Anya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
