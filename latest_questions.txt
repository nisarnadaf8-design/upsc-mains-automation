Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Ms. Ananya Sharma, a newly appointed IAS officer serving as the Sub-Divisional Magistrate in Vikaspur district, a rapidly urbanising region, is known for her meticulous approach to public administration. She has been tasked with overseeing the final stages of a critical land acquisition project for a proposed industrial corridor.

During her review, Ananya uncovers significant irregularities: undervaluation of farmers' land, inflated compensation for specific plots linked to a prominent private builder, and procedural shortcuts bypassing environmental assessments. Her superior, the District Collector Mr. Rakesh Kumar, advises her to 'expedite approvals' for the 'greater developmental good.' A powerful local MLA, Mr. Suresh Singh, also pressures her to ignore 'minor discrepancies,' citing political implications and potential career setbacks.

The farmers stand to lose their land and livelihood without fair compensation, while the private builders stand to gain illicit profits. Her decision will significantly impact public trust in governance and her own professional integrity and future career trajectory, regardless of the path she chooses.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
