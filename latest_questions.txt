Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Divya Sharma, a newly appointed Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) in the district town of Rudrapur, a bustling urban center with a history of communal sensitivities, is facing her first major challenge. A minor altercation during a religious procession has rapidly escalated, leading to widespread tension and sporadic clashes between two prominent communities. The situation threatens to spiral out of control, demanding immediate and impartial administrative intervention to restore peace and order.

As Divya prepares to deploy forces and initiate peace talks, she receives an insistent call from the powerful local Member of Legislative Assembly (MLA), who also holds a significant ministerial portfolio. The MLA explicitly instructs her to take stringent action, including arrests, primarily against individuals from one specific community, claiming it's crucial for maintaining 'order' and "political stability." He subtly warns of severe repercussions on her career if his 'guidance' is not followed. Simultaneously, the District Superintendent of Police, while personally advocating for a balanced approach, hints at the immense political pressure he is also under.

Divya's decision will directly impact the lives and fundamental rights of many citizens, the fragile communal harmony of Rudrapur, and the integrity of the district administration. Upholding impartiality could invite political wrath and potential career setbacks, while succumbing to pressure risks exacerbating violence, undermining justice, and eroding public faith in the rule of law.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Divya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
