Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Anjali Sharma, a probationary IAS officer, is posted as the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) in Devgarh District, State of Aryavarta. Her primary responsibility includes overseeing land records, revenue administration, and local development projects. Recently, she was tasked with the final review of a large land acquisition process for a proposed industrial corridor, a project championed by powerful private builders.

During her review, Anjali uncovers substantial irregularities: suppressed land values, dubious ownership transfers, and the forceful displacement of tribal and marginalized farmers without fair compensation, all seemingly orchestrated to benefit the private builders. Her immediate superior, the District Collector, subtly advises her to expedite the process without 'unnecessary scrutiny' citing 'larger public interest' and 'development targets'. A local political leader, with visible ties to the builders, also 'requests' her cooperation, hinting at potential career repercussions.

Her decision directly impacts hundreds of impoverished families facing homelessness and loss of livelihood, the credibility of the district administration, and the ethical foundations of public service. Both confirming the acquisition with irregularities and exposing them carry significant professional and personal consequences for Anjali.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Anjali Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
