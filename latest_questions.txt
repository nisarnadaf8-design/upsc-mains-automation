Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Ms. Ananya Sharma is the District Magistrate of Rudrapur District in Uttar Pradesh, a region known for its diverse population and historical communal harmony. Over the past year, a dormant land dispute between two communities has been reignited, exacerbated by provocative social media campaigns and local vested interests. Despite her efforts, the situation is increasingly tense, with sporadic clashes reported, creating a volatile environment.

As state assembly elections draw near, Ms. Sharma receives explicit instructions from the local Member of Parliament, who is also an influential cabinet minister. They demand that she take swift, biased action against one particular community, claiming it is necessary to restore order and will significantly aid the ruling party's electoral prospects. They subtly warn her of adverse career consequences, including a potential transfer to an insignificant post, if she fails to comply with their directive. Ms. Sharma is acutely aware that such a partisan move would violate constitutional principles, undermine due process, and likely inflame communal passions further, leading to greater instability.

Her decision will directly impact the safety, rights, and trust of both communities, the credibility of the district administration, and the integrity of the electoral process. Both compliance and defiance carry significant risks for public order, justice, and her professional future.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
