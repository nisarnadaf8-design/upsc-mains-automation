Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Officer Rohit Sharma, an upright IPS officer of the 2015 batch, is currently serving as the Superintendent of Police in the Anti-Corruption Bureau (ACB) of a major metropolitan city. For the past six months, his team has been meticulously investigating a large-scale land allotment scam involving illicit gains by a powerful real estate cartel. The investigation has progressed significantly, unearthing substantial evidence and public pressure for swift action is mounting.

During the final stages of compiling the evidence, Rohit discovers irrefutable proof that a few senior officers within his own police department, including his immediate superior, the Inspector General (IG) of ACB, have actively facilitated the scam through deliberate inaction and sharing of confidential information for personal enrichment. Exposing this would not only bring down his superiors but also severely tarnish the reputation and public trust in the police force he serves, creating immense pressure from within the department to bury the findings. A powerful state minister is also subtly hinting at adverse career consequences if the investigation proceeds in this direction.

His decision will profoundly impact the integrity of the police force, the public's faith in justice, the careers of his colleagues and superiors, and his own professional future. Both exposing and concealing the truth carry severe, far-reaching social, moral, and administrative ramifications.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rohit Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
