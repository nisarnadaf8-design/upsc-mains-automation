Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Rohan Sharma, a diligent District Environmental Officer in the State Pollution Control Board, has recently been transferred to the Peenya Industrial Area in Karnataka, a region critical for both industrial output and ecological balance. Known for his unwavering integrity and commitment to environmental laws, Rohan's primary responsibility is to ensure industrial compliance with pollution control norms.

Shortly after his arrival, Rohan's team uncovers clear evidence that "Vardhman Textiles," a prominent industry and a major employer in the area, is discharging untreated effluents directly into a local river. This practice has led to severe water contamination, causing widespread skin diseases and gastrointestinal issues among the villagers downstream. Rohan's superior, the Secretary of the Environment Department, discreetly advises a "pragmatic approach," emphasizing the company's contribution to employment and state revenue. Simultaneously, the local MLA directly calls Rohan, hinting at significant political repercussions if the matter is not "managed sensitively." The company itself threatens massive layoffs if forced to undertake expensive immediate remediation.

Rohan is acutely aware that overlooking the violations would severely impact public health and the environment, betraying his professional ethics and the trust placed in him. Conversely, strict enforcement risks significant political backlash and economic disruption, potentially jeopardizing his career and the livelihoods of many workers.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rohan Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
