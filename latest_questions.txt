Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ms. Anjali Sharma is the District Magistrate of Hoshangabad, a rapidly developing district in Madhya Pradesh. She is known for her impeccable integrity and efficiency. Currently, she is overseeing a flagship state project for large-scale public housing intended for economically weaker sections (EWS) on a substantial plot of land clearly designated for public utility in the city's master plan, with construction already underway.

A powerful State Cabinet Minister, Mr. Rajendra Singh, whose private land parcel is adjacent to the EWS housing site, approaches Ms. Sharma. He expresses his desire to acquire a significant portion of the public utility land for a nominal sum, stating it is for the "expansion of his educational trust's campus." This acquisition would necessitate an alteration to the approved master plan and the displacement of a substantial number of future EWS beneficiaries, causing considerable delays to the crucial housing project. Mr. Singh directly calls Ms. Sharma, subtly hinting at potential adverse career repercussions if she does not comply. Concurrently, her Principal Secretary advises her to "find a pragmatic way" to accommodate the minister, citing "higher political considerations." Meanwhile, rumours of the proposed land alteration have reached local community leaders, who are beginning to mobilise public opinion and threaten widespread protests if the EWS project is compromised.

Ms. Sharma’s decision will significantly impact not only the fate of the EWS housing project and its beneficiaries but also the credibility of public administration. Both acceding to the pressure and outright refusal carry serious consequences for the project, her career, and public trust.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case.
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Anjali Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits.
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved?
