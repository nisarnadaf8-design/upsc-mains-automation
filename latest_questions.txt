Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Rahul Kumar, a diligent District Development Officer (DDO) in Rudrapur District, Uttarakhand, is highly regarded for his commitment to public service and transparent administration. He recently oversaw a flagship rural road construction project which, through his proactive measures, was completed ahead of schedule and significantly under budget, earning widespread public appreciation and state recognition.

A local investigative journalist, known for uncovering corruption, has filed a Right to Information (RTI) application demanding comprehensive financial records, contractor payment details, and all internal audit reports for the successful road project. Simultaneously, Rahul's superior, the District Magistrate, informally advised him to be discreet, expressing concerns that releasing overly granular details, especially a specific internal audit note highlighting a minor procedural lapse already rectified, could be exploited by political rivals to discredit the project and the administration, potentially hindering future development initiatives.

The primary stakeholders include the RTI applicant, the local community benefiting from the roads, the contractors, Rahul's superior, and potentially political figures. Releasing all information could either solidify public trust or trigger unnecessary political controversy, while withholding it could protect the project from political attacks but risk eroding Rahul’s reputation for transparency and the public's faith in the system.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rahul Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
