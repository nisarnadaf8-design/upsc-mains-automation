Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Amit Sharma, a diligent and upright IPS officer, has recently taken charge as Superintendent of Police (SP) of a sensitive district in a rapidly urbanizing state. His mandate includes modernizing police infrastructure and tackling organized crime. During an internal audit of procurement for new surveillance equipment, anomalies were flagged.

Amit's investigation, initially into minor discrepancies, uncovers a significant procurement scam involving kickbacks and substandard equipment. Disturbingly, the evidence points towards involvement of several senior officers within his own department, including a powerful Deputy Inspector General (DIG) known to be close to a prominent state minister. Amit is pressured by his immediate superior to 'manage' the report to avoid embarrassment for the department and potential political fallout, while a local RTI activist group has also hinted at knowledge of irregularities and is demanding transparency.

Exposing the scam could lead to departmental upheaval, political backlash, threats to his career and personal safety, but upholding integrity would restore public faith and ensure justice. Covering it up would compromise his ethics, betray public trust, and perpetuate corruption, while also potentially being exposed later with severe consequences for his credibility.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Amit Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
