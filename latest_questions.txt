Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Anjali Sharma, a diligent District Magistrate in the urban development department of Zilla Nagar, is overseeing the tendering process for a critical urban housing project aimed at resettling slum dwellers. The project, valued at several hundred crores, is crucial for the city's infrastructure and the welfare of thousands of impoverished families. Strict environmental clearance and quality control standards have been stipulated in the tender documents to ensure sustainable development and safety.

Just as the final bids are being reviewed, Anjali receives a direct call from the Chief Secretary, who, under veiled pressure from a powerful state minister, strongly suggests that a particular construction firm, 'MegaBuild Enterprises,' should be awarded the contract. MegaBuild's bid, however, falls short on environmental compliance and uses substandard materials according to internal technical assessments. The minister has publicly endorsed MegaBuild, and local media have already begun speculating about potential political interference, while resident welfare associations are closely monitoring the project's transparency.

Anjali's decision will directly impact thousands of slum dwellers awaiting housing, the reputation of her department and the entire administration, the financial integrity of the project, and her own career. Awarding the contract to MegaBuild could lead to an environmental disaster and unsafe dwellings, while refusing could invite severe political retaliation and potential career setbacks.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Anjali Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
