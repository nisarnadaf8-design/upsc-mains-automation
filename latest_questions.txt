Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ms. Anjali Sharma, District Magistrate of Rudrapur, a rapidly developing district, is overseeing a critical land acquisition process for a major national highway project. The project promises significant economic growth but also involves resettlement of numerous small farmers and tribals from a sensitive ecological zone. She is known for her integrity and adherence to due process.

Suddenly, she receives direct pressure from the influential State Cabinet Minister for Infrastructure, Mr. Raghav Singh, to expedite the land acquisition by relaxing certain environmental impact assessment norms and fast-tracking compensation disbursement without thorough verification of land titles. Her immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, subtly advises her to adopt a 'pragmatic' approach, hinting at potential career repercussions if the project faces delays. Meanwhile, local community groups and environmental NGOs are closely monitoring the process, threatening protests if due diligence is compromised.

Anjali's decision will directly impact thousands of displaced families, the environmental integrity of the region, the public exchequer, and the credibility of the administrative machinery. Both compliance and resistance carry significant personal and professional risks for her.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Anjali Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
