Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Rajesh Kumar serves as the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) in the Revenue Department of Barmer District, a region particularly susceptible to flash floods in Rajasthan. Following an unprecedented cloudburst and subsequent inundation, Rajesh has been designated as the nodal officer for managing and distributing emergency relief funds and materials to thousands of displaced families. His immediate task involves ensuring timely provision of food, shelter, medical aid, and initial rehabilitation grants, with public expectations soaring for transparent and efficient delivery.

Amidst the chaos and critical need, Rajesh receives a discreet but firm instruction from the Chief Minister's Office, implicitly conveyed through his District Collector. The instruction suggests diverting a significant portion of the allocated flood relief funds towards a long-pending infrastructure project in a politically influential leader's constituency, which is comparatively less affected by the current floods. The Collector advises Rajesh to be "pragmatic" and understand the "political realities" for the sake of smooth administrative functioning and his future career prospects. Adding to his dilemma, his widowed mother recently expressed her concerns about his slow career growth and the increasing financial burden on their family, indirectly urging him to avoid confrontation with powerful figures.

The district's flood victims are still grappling with immense losses and require every rupee of the allocated funds for survival and recovery. Diverting these resources would directly compromise their well-being, potentially leading to further suffering, unrest, and a significant loss of public faith in government institutions.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rajesh Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved from the Chief Minister's Office, the District Collector, and his family? (150 words)
