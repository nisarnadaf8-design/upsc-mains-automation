Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ms. Aditi Sharma, a newly appointed District Collector in a remote, resource-rich tribal district in Chhattisgarh, is known for her diligence and pro-people approach. The district is home to several indigenous communities whose lives and livelihoods are deeply intertwined with the forests and traditional land use. A significant portion of the district is designated for a proposed mega-industrial corridor, a flagship project of the State Government aimed at attracting investments and creating jobs.

Recently, the State Government issued an executive order mandating expedited land acquisition and forest clearance procedures for the industrial corridor, bypassing certain provisions of the Forest Rights Act and PESA, citing "urgent public interest." This order comes directly from the Chief Secretary's office, with implied political pressure from the Chief Minister's secretariat to ensure rapid progress. However, implementing this order would directly displace thousands of tribal families, disrupt their traditional way of life, and severely impact their access to forest produce and sacred sites, potentially leading to widespread unrest. Ms. Sharma has received numerous petitions from tribal community leaders and local NGOs highlighting the potential human rights violations and ecological damage.

Her decision will significantly affect the tribal communities, the state's economic development agenda, the credibility of her administration, and potentially her own career. Both strict adherence to the government order and a defiant stance have serious, far-reaching consequences for all parties involved, including the district's long-term peace and stability.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Aditi Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
