Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Ms. Anjali Sharma, a young and upright Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) in District Rudrapur, is known for her proactive approach to governance and strict adherence to administrative norms. During a routine inspection of industrial units in her sub-division aimed at assessing compliance with labour laws and environmental regulations, her team visited a garment manufacturing factory.

To her dismay, she found clear evidence of child labour, with several underage children working in hazardous conditions. Further investigation revealed that the factory is owned by a powerful state cabinet minister, Mr. Rakesh Kumar, who also holds significant influence within the ruling party. Her immediate superior, the District Collector, subtly advised her to proceed cautiously, citing the political sensitivity and potential repercussions on her career, while the local media has started speculating about child labour in the region generally.

Anjali's decision will directly impact the exploited children, the factory owner, her career, the integrity of the administration, and public trust in the system. Both rigorous enforcement and selective leniency carry significant ethical and practical consequences.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Anjali Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
