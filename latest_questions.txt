Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Aarav Sharma, the District Magistrate of Shantipur, a district known for its diverse population, has been diligently working to maintain peace and foster inter-community harmony. Recently, a minor local dispute escalated into communal tension after provocative social media posts further inflamed sentiments, leading to widespread unrest in certain pockets of the district.

The situation demands swift and impartial action. However, Aarav receives explicit directives from a powerful state minister to selectively target one community during law enforcement operations and to downplay the role of certain influential individuals from the minister's political base, despite evidence suggesting their involvement. His immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, hints at political repercussions if these directives are not followed.

Both compliance and defiance would have serious implications for communal harmony, public trust in governance, justice, and Aarav's professional future. The local communities, law enforcement, district administration's credibility, and the political leadership are all key stakeholders in Aarav's impending decision.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Aarav Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
