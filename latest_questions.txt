Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ananya Sharma, a bright and idealistic young IAS officer, was recently posted as the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) in Vikas Nagar, a district earmarked for a major industrial corridor. Her initial months involved overseeing a crucial land acquisition process for a new expressway project, which promised significant economic growth and connectivity to the region.

During her review of land records and compensation assessments, Ananya noticed a pattern of irregularities: certain plots, mysteriously acquired by a prominent private builder just months prior, were being disproportionately overvalued, while compensation for genuinely poor farmers’ land was being undervalued. Her immediate superior, the District Collector, subtly advised her to expedite clearances, hinting at 'political sensitivities' and 'project deadlines'. A local MLA also made an informal visit, emphasizing the builder's 'crucial role' in regional development and subtly implying career consequences if the process was stalled.

Ananya's decision will directly impact thousands of marginalized farmers who stand to lose their only source of livelihood, the public exchequer facing undue financial burden, and the credibility of the administration. Both proceeding with the irregularities or exposing them carry significant professional and personal ramifications for her.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
