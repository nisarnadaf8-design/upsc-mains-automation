Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Arjun Kumar, a diligent Deputy Secretary in the Ministry of Rural Development, recently uncovered significant financial irregularities involving a large-scale rural housing scheme. His department is responsible for ensuring transparent allocation and utilization of funds across several states. Arjun meticulously documented the fraud, which involved inflated costs and siphoning off funds meant for vulnerable beneficiaries in remote districts, and submitted a detailed report to the Vigilance Section of his Ministry.

Instead of immediate action, Arjun finds himself isolated. His immediate superior, who was perceived to be close to some implicated contractors, subtly warns him to 'not rock the boat' and questions his judgment. Simultaneously, a senior political functionary, whose constituency would be affected by a probe, starts orchestrating a smear campaign against Arjun through anonymous leaks to the media, questioning his integrity and motives, and subtly hinting at a transfer to an insignificant post. His family is also distressed by the public scrutiny and potential career repercussions.

The key stakeholders are the defrauded rural beneficiaries, the integrity of the Ministry, the public's trust in governance, and Arjun's career and family well-being. Both pursuing the truth and backing down carry severe consequences for public accountability, personal reputation, and the welfare of many.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Arjun Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
