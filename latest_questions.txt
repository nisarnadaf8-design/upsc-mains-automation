Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Smita Sharma, a dedicated IAS officer of 2018 batch, serves as the District Magistrate of Sagar district, known for its vulnerability to monsoon floods. Following a recent catastrophic flood that devastated the region, she has been appointed as the Nodal Officer for the Prime Minister's Flood Relief Fund, overseeing the disbursement of crucial aid, including food, shelter, and medical supplies to thousands of displaced families in remote villages. Her team has meticulously assessed the immediate needs and prepared a disbursement plan.

Just as the first tranche of funds is about to be released, Smita receives a direct order from the state's Chief Minister's Office to reallocate a significant portion of the relief funds, earmarked for emergency housing, towards a politically sensitive infrastructure project in a neighboring constituency. Simultaneously, her immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, subtly advises her to comply to avoid political friction and potential career repercussions, hinting at the CM's personal interest. Local NGOs and affected communities, however, are desperately relying on the promised aid for their very survival.

Smita's decision will directly impact thousands of flood victims awaiting relief, the integrity of the relief operation, and public trust in government. Non-compliance could jeopardize her career and the broader relationship between the administration and the political executive, while compliance would betray her duty, compromise aid delivery, and potentially lead to further human suffering.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Smita Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
