Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Rajesh Kumar, an Under Secretary in the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, New Delhi, is tasked with handling various public information requests. Recently, his department received an RTI application seeking extensive details, including internal communication logs and specific file notings, related to the environmental clearance granted to a large-scale infrastructure project in an ecologically sensitive zone. The project, inaugurated by a senior political leader, is nearing completion.

The RTI applicant, a prominent environmental activist, alleges procedural irregularities and potential conflict of interest in the clearance process. While some information is publicly available, a significant portion of the requested details falls under 'cabinet papers' or 'commercial confidence' as per the Official Secrets Act and specific RTI exemptions. Rajesh's immediate superior, a Joint Secretary, has instructed him to release only the bare minimum public information, citing potential diplomatic implications and investment uncertainty if sensitive details are divulged. However, the activist has publicly threatened to expose perceived governmental opacity and malfeasance if full disclosure is not made.

Rajesh's decision will directly impact the activist's perception of government transparency, the credibility of the Ministry, the reputation of the involved political leader and investors, and potentially ignite further public debate on environmental governance. Non-disclosure might fuel public distrust, while full disclosure could trigger legal challenges, political backlash, and a loss of confidentiality essential for future policy discussions.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rajesh Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
