Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Divya Sharma, a diligent District Magistrate in Lakhimpur District, is overseeing a critical state highway expansion project aimed at improving connectivity and economic development in a remote region. The project involves significant land acquisition and adherence to stringent environmental clearances, which she has meticulously ensured thus far.

The State Minister for Infrastructure, a powerful political figure, has recently exerted immense pressure on Divya to alter a section of the approved highway alignment. The proposed diversion would pass through a small but ecologically sensitive forest patch, allegedly to enhance the value of certain private land parcels owned by the Minister's close associates, directly contravening environmental protection laws and the original detailed project report. Divya's immediate superior, the Chief Secretary, has also subtly hinted at accommodating the Minister's request to avoid political friction.

Divya's decision will directly impact local tribal communities living near the forest, the project's long-term environmental sustainability, public trust in governance, and her own professional integrity. Yielding to pressure risks legal repercussions and environmental degradation, while resisting might jeopardise her career and create administrative deadlock for the crucial development project.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Divya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
