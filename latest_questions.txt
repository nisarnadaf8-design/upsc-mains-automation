Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Rohan Sharma is the District Collector of Aranya District, a remote and ecologically sensitive region in a central Indian state. The Union Ministry of Environment has recently greenlighted the 'Green Corridor Highway Project', a national strategic initiative aimed at connecting major industrial hubs. A 20 km stretch of this highway is slated to pass through Aranya, necessitating the acquisition of ancestral lands belonging to the indigenous 'Vanwasi' community, a recognized linguistic minority group. These lands also house a sacred grove central to the Vanwasi people's faith and cultural identity. Rohan has been tasked with expediting the land acquisition process to ensure timely project completion.

Rohan faces immense pressure from his Chief Secretary to meet strict deadlines for the project, who emphasizes its national strategic importance and the significant economic benefits it promises. Concurrently, local community leaders, supported by a coalition of environmental activists, are staging peaceful protests, citing violations of their traditional forest rights, the irreversible destruction of their cultural heritage, and inadequate compensation based on their customary land valuation. A prominent political leader from an opposition party has publicly accused the district administration of callously disregarding tribal rights in favor of corporate interests, further escalating tensions.

The decision Rohan makes will directly impact the Vanwasi community's livelihood, cultural practices, and spiritual connection to their land. It will also significantly affect the environmental integrity of the region, the state's economic development trajectory, and Rohan's professional integrity and career prospects. Both proceeding with the acquisition and delaying or stalling it carry serious social, economic, and political consequences for multiple stakeholders.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rohan Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
