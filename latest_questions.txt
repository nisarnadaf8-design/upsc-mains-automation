Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Arjun Sharma, a District Magistrate in a tribal-dominated district of Chhattisgarh, has been tasked with overseeing the land acquisition process for a crucial national green energy corridor project. The project promises significant economic growth and energy security for the state, but requires the acquisition of ancestral lands from several indigenous communities who rely on forest resources for their livelihood.

Local community leaders, asserting their rights under PESA and Forest Rights Act, are vehemently opposing the acquisition, citing displacement, loss of cultural heritage, and inadequate rehabilitation packages. Arjun's superiors are pressing him to expedite the process, warning of severe project delays and cost escalations if he fails. Simultaneously, a powerful local political leader, with vested interests in the land deal, is subtly manipulating community sentiments against the project, while offering 'support' to Arjun in clearing hurdles if he concedes to certain demands.

Arjun's decision will directly affect the livelihoods and cultural identity of thousands of indigenous people, the strategic national interest of energy security, the district's economic future, and his own professional integrity. Both forceful acquisition and indefinite delay carry serious, far-reaching socio-economic and political consequences.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Arjun Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
