Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ms. Ananya Sharma, a recently appointed Assistant Collector in the Revenue Department of Rudrapur District, is known for her diligence and integrity. She is currently overseeing the land acquisition process for a crucial state highway bypass project aimed at easing traffic congestion and boosting regional commerce. Her team has meticulously surveyed the proposed route and identified all affected plots as per statutory guidelines.

A powerful senior MLA from the ruling party, Mr. Virendra Singh, owns a significant commercial property that falls directly in the original proposed path of the highway bypass. He has directly approached Ms. Sharma, demanding an immediate and substantial deviation in the highway alignment to save his property, even though such a change would violate existing engineering norms and significantly inflate project costs by affecting additional private lands unnecessarily. Ms. Sharma's immediate superior, the District Collector, has subtly advised her to "explore flexible options" given the political sensitivity and the urgency to complete the project without political roadblocks. Local public enthusiasm for the project is high, but the potential for public resentment due to rule-bending is also significant.

Ms. Sharma is faced with the dilemma of upholding the rule of law and her personal integrity versus yielding to political pressure to ensure project continuity and avoid potential career repercussions. Her decision will impact the fairness of the land acquisition process, the public exchequer, the credibility of the administration, and her own professional future.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
