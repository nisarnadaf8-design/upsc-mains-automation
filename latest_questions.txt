Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

SP Rohan Verma, a young and upright officer, was recently posted as Superintendent of Police in Mandar District. Known for his keen eye for detail and commitment to ethical policing, he initiated a review of ongoing infrastructure projects. During this review, he stumbled upon significant financial discrepancies in the multi-crore Police Welfare Housing Project, a flagship initiative from the previous administration.

His preliminary investigation reveals a well-orchestrated scam involving inflated bills and substandard materials, with clear indications that several senior officers, including the current Additional Director General of Police (ADGP) who was his predecessor in Mandar, are implicated. Exposing this would mean directly confronting his superior and potentially destabilizing the department, inviting political interference given the ADGP's connections. Rohan faces immense pressure to either overlook the findings for departmental harmony or expose the truth.

The decision will profoundly impact the morale and living conditions of police personnel, public trust in the administration, the careers of implicated officers, and Rohan’s own professional integrity. Both exposing and concealing the scam carry severe and far-reaching consequences for the institution and society.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rohan Verma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
