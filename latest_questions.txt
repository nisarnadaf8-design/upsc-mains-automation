Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Smt. Anjali Sharma, a diligent District Collector in the economically sensitive district of Raigarh, Chhattisgarh, is overseeing a major tribal welfare project. The project involves resettlement and rehabilitation of several villages due to a critical public infrastructure development. Her office is also responsible for land acquisition records and has been lauded for community engagement efforts.

An RTI applicant, representing a local tribal advocacy group, has sought complete records, including internal deliberations and preliminary land valuation reports, related to the project over the last three years. Simultaneously, a senior political leader has strongly advised Smt. Sharma to withhold certain 'sensitive' details, citing potential public unrest and delays that could derail the project and undermine ongoing negotiations with affected communities. Her immediate superior, concerned about the project's timeline and the political ramifications, subtly hints at classifying the requested information under 'official secrecy' provisions.

Her decision will directly impact the trust of the tribal communities, the transparency of governance, the project's legitimacy, and her own professional integrity. Both full disclosure and complete withholding of information carry significant socio-political and legal ramifications.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
The core ethical dilemmas here involve transparency vs. official secrecy, public accountability vs. maintaining social order/project timelines, upholding the rule of law (RTI Act) vs. succumbing to political pressure, and ensuring justice for vulnerable communities. Also, Smt. Sharma's professional integrity is at stake.

(b) What are the options available to Smt. Anjali Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
Option 1: Disclose all requested information. Merits: Upholds transparency, strengthens public trust, adheres to RTI's spirit. Demerits: Potential for public unrest, project delays, clash with political/administrative superiors.
Option 2: Withhold all 'sensitive' information. Merits: Avoids immediate conflict, maintains project pace. Demerits: Undermines transparency, erodes public trust, potential legal challenge under RTI, compromises ethical governance.
Option 3: Disclose information permissible under RTI Act, redacting exempted categories. Merits: Balances transparency and strategic interests, legally sound, protects vulnerable data. Demerits: May not fully satisfy applicant, requires meticulous legal justification.

(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
I recommend Option 3: Disclosing all information permissible under the RTI Act, meticulously redacting only those details explicitly exempted (e.g., information prejudicing project safety or personal privacy of tribal beneficiaries during ongoing negotiations). This balances transparency with responsible governance and legal compliance.
My justification is that it respects the citizen's right to information, maintains official integrity, and proactively addresses public concerns while safeguarding critical project interests.
To handle pressures: I would formally document the RTI response, citing specific legal provisions for any redactions, informing superiors. I would politely but firmly explain the legal mandate of RTI to political leaders, emphasizing the long-term benefits of transparency in building trust and ensuring project success. I would also initiate open dialogue sessions with tribal community representatives to clarify the disclosure process and address their specific grievances, thereby diffusing potential unrest through direct engagement.
