Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Anjali Sharma, a diligent District Magistrate, is posted in Ratanpur, a district with a history of communal sensitivities. Ahead of a major religious procession, a minor altercation between two community groups has quickly escalated, creating palpable tension and fear across the region. As the lead administrator, Anjali is responsible for maintaining peace and ensuring law and order.

Amidst this volatile situation, a powerful local political leader from the ruling party approaches Anjali, demanding that FIRs be selectively filed against members of one community and that permissions for the upcoming procession be withheld for the other. The leader explicitly states that compliance would ensure her smooth career progression, while defiance could lead to an immediate punitive transfer. Her immediate superior also subtly advises her to 'handle the political sensitivities with care'.

Anjali's decision will directly impact the safety, trust, and fundamental rights of both communities, the integrity of the district administration, and the rule of law. Both acting on the political demands or strictly upholding impartiality carry severe immediate and long-term consequences for the district and her career.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Anjali Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
