Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Ms. Ananya Sharma is the District Collector of Jalgaon, a drought-prone district in Maharashtra, known for her integrity and efficiency. Recently, a major state-funded water conservation project, 'Jal Sanjeevani Abhiyan,' was implemented in her district to construct check dams and deepen water bodies, involving substantial public funds and several private contractors. The project is critical for ensuring water security in the region and has directly benefited thousands of farmers.

An active citizen's group, "Jal Shakti Manch," led by a prominent activist, Mr. Rajesh Mehta, files a detailed Right to Information (RTI) application seeking comprehensive project records, including contractor selection criteria, payment schedules, quality audit reports, and internal correspondence related to specific technical specifications. While Ms. Sharma firmly believes in transparency and accountability, her Divisional Commissioner has privately advised her to withhold certain details, particularly those related to preliminary internal inquiries into alleged irregularities and confidential contractual clauses, citing "public interest" to prevent disruption of ongoing work and potential legal challenges. A local political leader has also subtly pressured her to delay full disclosure, emphasizing the need for 'administrative stability' and avoiding 'unnecessary controversy' that could undermine the government's efforts.

Ms. Sharma's decision will directly impact the RTI applicant's trust, the public's perception of accountability, the morale of her team, and the integrity of future government projects. Both granting full information, potentially revealing sensitive ongoing investigations or proprietary contractor details, and invoking official secrecy could have significant legal, political, and social ramifications, affecting either public trust or administrative efficacy.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
