Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ms. Anjali Sharma, a diligent IAS officer serving as the Sub-Divisional Magistrate in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, was renowned for her commitment to social welfare initiatives. During a routine inspection of local industries following a surge in school dropouts in a specific area, her team received a tip-off about a garment factory.

Upon a surprise visit, Ms. Sharma's team discovered multiple underage children working in hazardous conditions at 'Shakti Garments'. Further investigation revealed the factory was owned by Mr. Devendra Singh, a powerful local MLA known for his political clout and close ties to the state's ruling party. Soon after the discovery, Ms. Sharma received a discreet call from the Chief Secretary's office, advising "prudence" and "political sensitivity" in the matter, followed by an indirect warning from Mr. Singh's office about potential career repercussions.

The children, their families, and the integrity of the administrative system are direct stakeholders. Acting against the factory risks political backlash, transfer, or stalled career prospects for Ms. Sharma, while inaction would perpetuate child exploitation, violate human rights, and severely compromise her ethical standing and public trust in governance.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Anjali Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
