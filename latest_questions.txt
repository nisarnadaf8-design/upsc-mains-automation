Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Ms. Ananya Sharma is the District Magistrate of Sargam, a tribal-dominated district in a resource-rich state, overseeing land acquisition for a critical national infrastructure project – a major dam and power plant, designated as a Project of National Importance. Her district is central to the project's success, which promises significant economic benefits and energy security for the nation.

The local Gond tribal communities, who have inhabited the project area for generations, are vehemently opposing the acquisition, asserting their ancestral rights over the forest land based on cultural sanctity and unique ecological dependence. They are demanding rehabilitation in equivalent forest land within the same region and a share in the project's benefits, which far exceeds the standard compensation package and poses a significant budgetary challenge. The Principal Secretary has issued a strict directive to Ms. Sharma to expedite the process, warning against any delays that could inflate project costs, while a powerful local political leader is actively instigating protests, accusing the administration of anti-tribal policies.

Her decision will directly affect the lives and livelihoods of thousands of tribal families, the financial viability of a strategic national project, and the perception of the state's commitment to both development and tribal welfare. Both immediate forceful action and prolonged indecision carry severe social, economic, and political consequences for the region and the nation.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case.
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits.
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved?
