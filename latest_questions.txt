Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ranjit Kumar, a dedicated IAS officer, serves as the District Magistrate of 'Nalanda' district, a region recently designated as a Special Economic Zone (SEZ). He is known for his strict adherence to rules and transparent administration, which has earned him respect but also some adversaries. The state elections are merely three months away.

The ruling political party's influential local MLA approaches Ranjit with a demand to fast-track the issuance of land ownership certificates to certain individuals and entities, many of whom do not fully meet the eligibility criteria. The MLA explicitly states that these beneficiaries are crucial to the party's election campaign and any delay or refusal would be seen as an act against the party's interests, with veiled threats regarding Ranjit's career progression. His immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, subtly advises him to "manage the situation pragmatically" to avoid political friction.

Ranjit's decision will impact not only his personal integrity and career but also the legitimate landowners, the credibility of the district administration, the rule of law, and potentially the outcome of the elections and future development of the SEZ. Both compliance and defiance carry significant and complex ramifications.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ranjit Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
