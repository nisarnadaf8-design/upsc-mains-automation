Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Ms. Anya Sharma, a bright and idealistic young IAS officer, is serving as the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) in a rapidly urbanising district. Her current responsibility involves overseeing the land acquisition process for a crucial highway bypass project. This project promises significant economic growth but requires the acquisition of substantial agricultural and private land parcels.

During her meticulous review of land records and compensation proposals, Anya uncovers glaring irregularities. She finds instances of deliberate undervaluation of fertile agricultural land, manipulation of ownership documents, and an unusually swift approval process for certain parcels disproportionately benefiting a powerful private real estate developer with strong political connections. Her immediate superior, the District Magistrate, advises her to not dwell on minor discrepancies, stressing the project's strategic importance and political urgency. A local MLA has also subtly conveyed the political ramifications of delaying the project.

Farmers, many belonging to vulnerable communities, face imminent displacement with inadequate compensation, while the private developer stands to gain immense profits. Anya's decision will directly impact the lives and livelihoods of thousands, the financial health of the state exchequer, the integrity of her office, and her own career trajectory.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Anya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
