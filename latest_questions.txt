Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Anjali Sharma, a diligent and upright IAS officer, has recently taken charge as the District Magistrate of Purna, a district historically prone to sporadic communal incidents, especially during religious festivals. A major religious procession is scheduled next week, and intelligence reports suggest heightened communal tensions, necessitating careful management of the route and permissions.

The ruling party's influential local MLA, who is also known to be close to the Chief Minister, directly instructs Anjali to allow a deviation from the agreed procession route for one community, while simultaneously insisting on strict punitive action against any minor infraction by the other community. The MLA explicitly states this is crucial for maintaining political stability and electoral advantage, subtly implying consequences if her instructions are not followed.

Anjali's decision will directly impact public safety, the fairness of administration, communal harmony, and the credibility of the district administration. Both compliance and defiance carry significant risks, from immediate public disorder to long-term career repercussions.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
The key ethical issues include upholding the rule of law and impartiality, maintaining political neutrality, preserving public trust in administration, integrity, courage of conviction, and ensuring social justice and communal harmony, despite political pressure for biased action.

(b) What are the options available to Anjali Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
Options available to Anjali Sharma include:
1. Complying with the MLA's instructions: Merits – avoids immediate political confrontation, potential career stability. Demerits – gravely compromises rule of law, impartiality, public trust; fuels communal tension, and is morally indefensible.
2. Defying instructions and enforcing rules impartially: Merits – upholds constitutional values, ensures justice, prevents communal escalation, reinforces administrative integrity. Demerits – high risk of political reprisal, transfer, and strained executive relations.
3. Engaging with the MLA to explain legal implications and potential consequences of biased action, while proposing an alternative, impartial enforcement strategy. Merits – attempts persuasion, seeks middle ground, protects integrity. Demerits – success isn't guaranteed; still risks political displeasure if persuasion fails.

(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
I would recommend Anjali Sharma to firmly uphold the rule of law and ensure impartial enforcement of regulations for both communities. This course of action, while challenging, is essential to maintain public trust, prevent communal violence, and preserve the integrity and neutrality of the administration. It aligns with the constitutional ethos of justice and equality.

To handle the pressures, Anjali should:
1. Document all communication from the political executive, ensuring a clear record of the instructions received.
2. Immediately apprise her immediate superior, the Chief Secretary, about the political interference and seek official guidance, emphasizing the potential for law and order breakdown.
3. Publicly reiterate the administration's commitment to strict and impartial enforcement for all, engaging all community leaders equally to build confidence.
4. Prepare contingency plans with the police force to manage any fallout, ensuring sufficient deployment and intelligence gathering.
5. Be prepared for potential political retaliation, demonstrating courage of conviction, understanding that upholding dharma is paramount over personal career considerations.
