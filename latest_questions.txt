Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Smt. Kavya Sharma, a young and earnest IAS officer of the 2023 batch, was serving as the District Magistrate of Sagar District in Uttar Pradesh. The district had been severely hit by unprecedented floods in July 2026, leading to widespread displacement, significant loss of livelihoods, and damage to critical infrastructure. Kavya was personally overseeing the procurement and distribution of emergency relief supplies, including crucial medical equipment, temporary shelters, and essential food items for thousands of affected families.

During a routine, unannounced inspection of a major relief godown, Kavya uncovered glaring discrepancies in the procurement documents for high-value medical supplies. Further discreet but thorough investigation confirmed that her immediate superior, Shri Alok Kumar, a seasoned Principal Secretary in the state administration, had colluded with a specific vendor to siphon off a significant portion of the relief funds. This was achieved by approving substandard goods at highly inflated prices. The crucial supplies meant for the flood victims were severely compromised in quantity and quality, directly impacting the effectiveness of aid reaching the suffering populace and putting lives at risk.

Kavya is now caught in a profound ethical dilemma. Exposing her influential senior colleague could jeopardize her nascent career, potentially leading to institutional backlash, transfers, and personal threats, while remaining silent would be a gross compromise of her integrity, a betrayal of public trust, and would directly endanger the lives and well-being of thousands of flood-affected citizens who depend on unadulterated, timely relief.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Smt. Kavya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
