Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Divya Sharma, a newly appointed District Magistrate in the disaster-prone district of Rudraprayag, Uttarakhand, is spearheading critical relief and rehabilitation efforts following unprecedented monsoon floods and landslides. The situation is dire, with thousands displaced, infrastructure severely damaged, and immediate aid crucial for survival. She is working tirelessly, coordinating various agencies and aid deliveries to remote areas.

During a routine audit of emergency procurement records, Divya uncovers irregularities indicating that her immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, Mr. Rakesh Verma, has approved inflated invoices for relief material from certain contractors. Further investigation reveals a significant portion of these funds has been siphoned off. Mr. Verma is politically well-connected and has hinted at 'flexible accounting' being necessary for 'smooth operations' in the past. Reporting him could jeopardize the ongoing relief work, invite political retaliation, and potentially damage her own career, while inaction would allow the embezzlement to continue, directly impacting the suffering population.

The primary stakeholders are the disaster-affected population, who are being denied essential aid, and the public's trust in governance. Divya's colleagues, the administration's integrity, and her personal career also stand to be gravely affected. Both immediate action and continued silence carry severe and far-reaching consequences for human lives and ethical governance.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Divya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
