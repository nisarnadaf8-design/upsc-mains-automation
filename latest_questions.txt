Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Ms. Aditi Singh, a probationary IAS officer of the 2024 batch, is posted as the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) in the rapidly urbanizing district of Sunderpur. Her primary responsibility includes overseeing land acquisition processes for various infrastructure projects, a key focus for the state government aiming for rapid industrial growth and urban expansion.

During a review of a major land acquisition file for a proposed satellite city project, Aditi discovers glaring discrepancies. Compensation rates for fertile agricultural land belonging to small farmers are drastically undervalued, while strategically located adjacent barren plots, recently acquired by a prominent private builder, 'Harmony Developers,' show inflated valuations. Her immediate superior, the District Collector, Mr. Rajeev Mehta, advises her to expedite the file, subtly hinting at political pressure from a powerful state minister and the builder lobby to avoid 'unnecessary delays' that could impact her career trajectory.

The decision will directly affect hundreds of marginalized farmers, the credibility of the district administration, the financial interests of 'Harmony Developers,' and the integrity of the land acquisition process. Both approving the flawed acquisition and raising objections carry significant consequences for public trust and Aditi's professional future.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Aditi Singh? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
