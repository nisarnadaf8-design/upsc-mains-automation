Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Avantika Sharma, a recently promoted District Environmental Officer in the State Pollution Control Board, is posted in Vardhmanpur, a major industrial district. Known for her integrity, she finds herself in a challenging environment where older industries, crucial for local employment, often struggle to meet modern environmental norms.

A recent inspection confirmed 'Bharat Chemicals Ltd.', a prominent and influential industry and major employer, is illegally discharging untreated toxic effluents into the local river, severely impacting downstream agriculture and potable water sources. Avantika's Chairman subtly advises "pragmatic flexibility" citing the company's economic contributions and political patronage. The local MLA has also personally conveyed expectations of "understanding" to prevent industrial unrest, while her own sibling is employed by a firm that supplies Bharat Chemicals Ltd.

The local community, farmers, and environmental activists are demanding strict action, threatening protests. Her decision will significantly impact public health, environmental integrity, local livelihoods, the company's operations, and the credibility of the State Pollution Control Board, besides her personal and professional future.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Avantika Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
