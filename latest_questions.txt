Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Divya Sharma, a diligent District Welfare Officer in Bharatpur, an aspirational district, has been tasked with compiling the first quarterly impact report for the "Gramin Samriddhi Abhiyan," a flagship state welfare scheme for rural economic upliftment. Her ground teams have collected comprehensive data, which, while showing some positive trends, also highlights significant implementation bottlenecks and lower-than-projected beneficiary saturation.

As Divya prepares the draft report, she is summoned by the District Collector, who, under implicit pressure from the local Member of Legislative Assembly (MLA), suggests "optimizing" the data. The Collector explicitly asks her to inflate beneficiary numbers, embellish success stories, and downplay critical gaps, stating that a highly positive report is crucial for securing further state funding and political goodwill. Divya knows manipulating the data would present a misleading picture, compromising the scheme's integrity and future course corrections.

Her decision will directly affect thousands of genuine rural beneficiaries who rely on accurate scheme evaluation for effective resource allocation. It will also impact the credibility of government reporting, the ethical fabric of her department, and her personal integrity, setting a precedent for future administrative conduct. Both compliance and defiance carry significant professional and ethical ramifications.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Divya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
