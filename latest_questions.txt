Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Smt. Priya Sharma, a diligent IAS officer, is serving as the District Relief Coordinator in a coastal district recently devastated by unprecedented monsoon floods. Her primary responsibility is to ensure the swift and transparent distribution of emergency relief funds and materials to thousands of displaced families now living in temporary shelters. The state government has allocated a substantial corpus, and public expectations for rapid rehabilitation are extremely high.

Amidst the urgent relief operations, Priya receives a discreet but firm instruction from the Chief Minister's Office, conveyed through her immediate superior, the District Collector. The instruction is to quietly divert 20% of the allocated relief funds towards a newly proposed 'urban beautification project' in the state capital, a pet project of a senior minister, with a promise of career advancement for her. Concurrently, local media reports highlight increasing discontent among flood victims regarding delays and insufficient aid.

Priya's decision will directly impact thousands of vulnerable flood victims who desperately need aid for survival and rebuilding. It will also affect public trust in the administration, her career trajectory, and the integrity of the disaster relief mechanism. Both compliance and defiance carry severe personal and professional consequences.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Smt. Priya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
