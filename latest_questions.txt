Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Mr. Alok Kumar, the newly appointed District Collector of Rudrapradesh, a tribal-majority district rich in mineral resources, is grappling with the implementation of a state-sanctioned large-scale hydroelectric project. The project promises significant economic growth for the state, but is set to displace several indigenous tribal communities from their ancestral lands and traditional forest-based livelihoods.

He has received clear directives from the Chief Secretary to expedite land acquisition and resettlement processes to meet stringent project deadlines, emphasizing the state's development priorities. However, a recent survey by local tribal rights activists and his own ground visits reveal severe inadequacies in the proposed compensation and rehabilitation package, threatening irreversible damage to the tribal culture and ecosystem. The local MLA is also pressuring him to comply with the state's orders due to political stakes.

His decision will directly impact thousands of tribal families, the state's economic progress, and his own administrative credibility. Proceeding hastily risks social unrest and human rights violations, while delaying could lead to severe penalties from the state government and project cost escalations.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Mr. Alok Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
