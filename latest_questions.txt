Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Rohan Sharma is the newly appointed District Magistrate of Shantinagar, a region recently devastated by unprecedented monsoon floods. He arrived a week ago to oversee critical relief and rehabilitation efforts, with public infrastructure destroyed and lakhs displaced. The government has allocated substantial emergency funds for immediate aid and reconstruction.

During his review of procurement records for relief materials and temporary shelter construction, Rohan uncovers significant discrepancies. Evidence strongly suggests his immediate senior, Divisional Commissioner Mr. Alok Singh, is colluding with contractors to siphon off a substantial portion of these critical funds. Reporting this immediately would undoubtedly halt crucial relief operations temporarily and expose a powerful figure, while Mr. Singh has subtly hinted at his strong political connections and severe career repercussions if his activities are questioned. Local non-governmental organisations are independently raising concerns about the quality and delayed delivery of essential relief supplies.

The lives of flood victims, including countless women, children, and elderly, depend on the immediate and effective flow of aid. Rohan's decision will critically affect the integrity of governance, public trust, and potentially his own future in the civil service. Both immediate action and continued inaction have serious, far-reaching consequences.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rohan Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
