Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

SP Vikram Singh, a diligent and upright Indian Police Service officer of the 2015 batch, currently serves as the Deputy Commissioner of Police (DCP) in the Anti-Corruption Bureau (ACB) of the State Police in the bustling capital city of 'Vardhaman'. Known for his integrity and investigative acumen, he has recently spearheaded several high-profile arrests, significantly boosting public confidence in the agency.

During a routine review of an ongoing investigation into irregularities in public infrastructure tenders, Vikram uncovers a disturbing trail of evidence indicating a sophisticated network of kickbacks. To his shock, the evidence points towards direct involvement of several senior officers within his own ACB unit, including individuals close to the Director General of Police (DGP), in collusion with a powerful construction conglomerate. He faces immense pressure from a political heavyweight, subtly conveyed through a high-ranking official, to 'manage' the information and avoid an internal scandal that could destabilize the government.

Vikram's decision will directly affect the integrity of the state police, the faith of the common citizen in governance, the financial health of public projects, and his own career and moral standing. Both exposing and suppressing the evidence carry profound and far-reaching implications for public trust and accountability.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to SP Vikram Singh? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
