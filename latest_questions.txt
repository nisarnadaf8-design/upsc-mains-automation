Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Rohan Sharma, a relatively new recruit to the Indian Administrative Service, serves as the District Project Officer for the State Skill Development Mission in Chandpur, a remote and underdeveloped district. He has been diligently working to implement a crucial government initiative aimed at providing vocational training and employment opportunities to thousands of marginalized youth, earning him respect among the local populace and his team.

Just weeks before the crucial state assembly elections, Rohan is approached by the powerful local MLA, Mr. Ashok Verma, and his immediate superior, the District Collector. They exert immense pressure on him to issue 'skill completion' certificates to over 500 individuals who are known party workers but have not actually completed the prescribed training modules. The MLA explicitly states this is crucial for the party's election narrative, while the Collector hints at severe career repercussions, including adverse performance appraisals and punitive transfers, if Rohan fails to comply.

Rohan’s decision will profoundly impact the credibility of the Skill Development Mission, the future of genuine trainees who are competing for limited opportunities, and the integrity of the electoral process. Compliance would betray public trust and his professional ethics, while refusal could jeopardize his career and personal well-being.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rohan Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
