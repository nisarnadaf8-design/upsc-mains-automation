Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Ritu Sharma, a dynamic young District Magistrate of Kolar, a mineral-rich district, has a reputation for integrity and efficiency. She is new to the district but has been actively engaging with local communities and businesses to understand ground realities. Her recent focus has been on ensuring compliance with labour laws in the region's burgeoning manufacturing sector, especially following media reports of poor working conditions.

During a routine, unannounced inspection of a textile manufacturing unit, "Glorious Garments," Ritu's team uncovers clear evidence of child labour, with minors aged 10-14 working in hazardous and unregulated conditions. Further confidential investigations reveal that the factory is clandestinely owned by Prakash Singh, a powerful state cabinet minister from the ruling party, who also happens to be a close confidante of the Chief Minister. Ritu's immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, hints at "political sensitivities" and advises extreme caution, suggesting a "less confrontational" approach to avoid embarrassment to the government.

The children and their families, the factory workers, the minister, the district administration's credibility, the ruling party's image, and the larger issue of child labour enforcement are all profoundly affected. Any decision Ritu takes will have significant repercussions, both personal and professional for her, and for the cause of justice in the district.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ritu Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
