Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ms. Ananya Sharma, an upright and recently appointed District Magistrate of Rudrapur District in Uttarakhand, has earned a reputation for her commitment to social justice and administrative efficiency. During a routine, unannounced inspection spearheaded by her office's Child Welfare Unit, a shocking discovery was made in a large textile manufacturing unit, "Himalayan Looms," a prominent local employer in the district.

The inspection team found irrefutable evidence of systematic child labour, with several minors working in hazardous conditions. Further investigation revealed that "Himalayan Looms" is owned by Mr. Ved Prakash, a senior and highly influential cabinet minister in the state government. Immediately, Ms. Sharma received veiled warnings from the Chief Minister's Office suggesting the matter be handled "discreetly" to avoid political embarrassment, while her immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, subtly advised a "pragmatic approach" citing her promising career trajectory. Local media outlets, having caught wind of the inspection, are beginning to raise pointed questions.

Ms. Sharma faces an acute ethical dilemma. Her decision will directly impact the exploited children, their impoverished families, the rule of law, her own career, the integrity of the civil service, and public trust in governance. Both decisive action and cautious inaction carry significant and far-reaching consequences for all involved stakeholders.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
