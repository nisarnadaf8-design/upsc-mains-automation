========================================

Q1. (10 Marks | 150 words)

Q2. (10 Marks | 150 words)

Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ms. Ananya Sharma, a diligent IPS officer of the 2015 batch, is currently serving as the Superintendent of Police (Vigilance) in the State Police Housing Corporation, located in a bustling state capital. Her current assignment involves overseeing the quality and financial integrity of various infrastructure projects, including new police stations and residential quarters, a critical responsibility given the substantial public funds involved.

During a routine audit, Ananya uncovers undeniable evidence of a deep-rooted scam involving the use of substandard materials and fraudulent billing for several major projects. The evidence strongly implicates multiple senior officers within the Police Housing Corporation and, disturbingly, points towards complicity from officials in the Home Department, including those close to the Home Minister. Her Director General of Police has subtly advised her to proceed cautiously, hinting at the potential damage to the police force's image and her own promising career if she pursues the matter aggressively, while a local newspaper is independently investigating similar allegations.

Her decision will profoundly impact the integrity of the police force, the safety of officers residing in compromised buildings, public trust in governance, and potentially her personal safety and career trajectory. Both exposing and suppressing the scam carry severe, far-reaching consequences for all involved.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
