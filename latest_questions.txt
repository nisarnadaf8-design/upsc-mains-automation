GS-1 SOCIETY: Health: Public Health System and Social Determinants
========================================

Q1. (10 Marks | 150 words)
Examine the pervasive influence of deep-rooted socio-cultural norms and power dynamics, rather than merely economic factors, in exacerbating health disparities and hindering the effective implementation of public health initiatives across diverse Indian communities.

Q2. (10 Marks | 150 words)
What do you understand by the concept of 'health equity' in the context of India's evolving healthcare landscape? Critically assess how far national health programs are succeeding in narrowing the urban-rural divide and ensuring equitable access to comprehensive care, including mental health services.

Q3. (15 Marks | 250 words)
Critically analyse the complex interlinkages between gender, nutrition, and economic productivity in perpetuating cycles of poverty and ill-health in India, as evidenced by recent national health surveys. Discuss the social, economic, and policy interventions required to foster intergenerational well-being and break these cycles.

Q4. (10 Marks | 150 words)

Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ms. Anjali Sharma, a dedicated District Environmental Officer with the State Pollution Control Board in the rapidly industrializing district of Sunderpur, has consistently upheld environmental regulations. Her office is currently reviewing the expansion application of 'EcoTech Industries', a major employer and taxpayer in the region, known for its political connections.

During her team's inspection, significant non-compliance and unmitigated effluent discharge exceeding permissible limits were discovered at EcoTech's existing plant. However, her immediate superior, under subtle pressure from a powerful state minister and with veiled threats of departmental reshuffle, has instructed Anjali to expedite the approval by overlooking these violations. Simultaneously, local community groups have started protesting against the alleged pollution.

Anjali's decision will directly impact the health of thousands of residents, the local ecosystem, EcoTech's operations, her career, and the integrity of the regulatory body. Both approving the expansion or strictly enforcing regulations carry severe and far-reaching consequences.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Anjali Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
