Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ms. Ananya Sharma is the newly appointed Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) in a remote district of a politically sensitive state. Known for her integrity and commitment to public service, she has been actively working to streamline administrative processes. With general elections approaching, the political climate in her district is highly charged.

Her immediate superior, the District Collector, along with a powerful local political leader from the ruling party, urgently calls a meeting. Ananya is strongly urged to expedite and issue a large number of 'domicile certificates' and 'income certificates' to specific individuals, who are known supporters of the political party. It is clear to Ananya that many of these individuals do not meet the stipulated eligibility criteria. The Collector subtly hints at potential career repercussions, including an undesirable transfer, if she fails to cooperate, framing it as "maintaining good relations with stakeholders for smooth administration."

Ananya’s decision in this situation will significantly affect her personal integrity, the credibility of the revenue administration, the fairness of the electoral process, and the public's trust in governance. Both immediate compliance and outright refusal carry serious consequences for her career and the broader administrative setup.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
