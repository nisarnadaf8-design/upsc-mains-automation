Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Ms. Ananya Sharma, a diligent District Magistrate in Shahjahanpur district, is overseeing a critical express highway project. The project involves acquisition of agricultural land and requires strict adherence to environmental impact assessment norms as per a recent High Court directive. Her reputation for integrity and efficiency is well-known in the state administration.

During the final phase of land acquisition, a powerful State Minister, Mr. Rakesh Kumar, approaches Ms. Sharma directly. He insists on fast-tracking clearances for a specific section of land owned by a close associate, proposing to bypass certain environmental scrutiny steps and relax compensation norms for a particular village. He clearly communicates that her 'cooperation' is essential for her future career progression. Simultaneously, her immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, subtly advises her to "find a pragmatic solution" to avoid political friction, implying that strict adherence to rules might lead to unnecessary bureaucratic hurdles for the project.

Ms. Sharma's decision will directly impact hundreds of villagers whose livelihoods depend on the land, the project's environmental sustainability, and the state exchequer. Bending the rules could lead to public protests and legal challenges, undermining the project's legitimacy and the integrity of the administration. Upholding the rules might lead to her own transfer or career stagnation and earn the minister's wrath.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
