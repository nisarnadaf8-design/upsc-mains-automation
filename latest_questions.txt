Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Aparna Sharma is the newly posted District Collector of Chandrapur, a district rich in mineral resources, particularly bauxite. The state government has fast-tracked a large-scale bauxite mining project, promising significant revenue generation and job creation for the region. However, the proposed mining lease area encompasses vast tracts of ancestral forest land that are home to the particularly vulnerable 'Dongaria Kondh' tribal community, who depend on these forests for their livelihood, cultural identity, and spiritual beliefs, and hold valid Forest Rights Act claims.

Aparna receives a direct order from the Chief Secretary's office to expedite the land acquisition process and grant all necessary clearances for the mining project within a stringent timeline, citing the project's strategic economic importance. Simultaneously, she is faced with widespread protests and representations from the Dongaria Kondh community, supported by local activists, pleading for the protection of their ancestral lands and traditional way of life. The local MLA, who has significant business interests linked to the mining company, is also exerting considerable political pressure on her to comply with the state's directives, while a human rights commission has recently issued an advisory highlighting concerns about potential tribal displacement and loss of cultural heritage.

The tribal community faces potential displacement, irreversible loss of livelihood, and cultural disruption if the project proceeds without proper safeguards and consent. The state government stands to lose significant investment and political capital if the project is stalled, potentially impacting other development initiatives. Aparna's career and reputation are also on the line, as both strict compliance and outright defiance carry severe professional repercussions, impacting public trust and administrative efficacy simultaneously.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Aparna Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
