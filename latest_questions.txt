Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Aarav Sharma, a young and idealistic IAS officer, recently posted as Sub-Divisional Magistrate in the rapidly urbanizing district of Krishnapuram, is overseeing a major land acquisition project for a proposed industrial corridor. The project aims to boost regional development and create employment.

During the verification process, Aarav uncovers a pattern of discrepancies in land records and compensation assessments, strongly suggesting that large tracts of fertile agricultural land are being undervalued and acquired from vulnerable farmers, only to be resold immediately to a cartel of private builders at exorbitant prices. His immediate superior, the District Collector, subtly advises him to expedite the approvals without 'unnecessary scrutiny', hinting at political pressure from a powerful state minister who has a known association with the builder lobby.

Aarav's decision will directly impact thousands of farmers who face displacement and livelihood loss, as well as the district's economic development trajectory. His actions or inactions will also shape public trust in the administration and his own career trajectory.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Aarav Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
