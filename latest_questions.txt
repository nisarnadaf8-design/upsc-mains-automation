Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Divya Sharma, a diligent Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) of Brahmapuri district, has been entrusted with overseeing the distribution of crucial relief funds following unprecedented floods that have devastated her region. The district, predominantly rural and economically vulnerable, faces immense challenges in rebuilding lives and livelihoods, and public expectation for immediate and fair assistance is extraordinarily high. Divya is known for her impeccable record of integrity and dedication to public service.

Amidst the chaos and urgent need, Divya faces significant pressure from various powerful quarters. A local Member of Legislative Assembly (MLA) subtly suggests diverting a substantial portion of funds to a 'rehabilitation project' spearheaded by a politically connected contractor in his constituency, implying future career benefits. Simultaneously, her superior, the Principal Secretary, verbally instructs her to prioritise 'long-term infrastructure repair' over immediate direct aid, a move that could benefit certain large construction firms and indirectly involve kickbacks for higher officials. Meanwhile, local community leaders are vehemently demanding the immediate disbursement of direct relief to the suffering populace.

Divya understands that diverting funds would directly impact thousands of flood-affected families awaiting aid, deepening their misery and eroding public trust in the administration. However, resisting these pressures could lead to severe professional repercussions, including punitive transfers or career stagnation, and even potential political targeting. Both compliance and defiance carry heavy consequences for her career, the affected citizens, and the integrity of public service.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Divya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
