Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Rajeev Sharma, a recently posted Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) in the Revenue Department of a politically sensitive district, is known for his integrity and efficiency. He is keen to make a positive impact and streamline the issuance of various public certificates, a critical service for the local populace, especially with state elections approaching within the year.

A powerful local MLA and a senior political leader of the ruling party approaches Rajeev, insisting that he issue a large number of income and domicile certificates to individuals known to be ineligible, citing "party interests" and the need to secure votes for upcoming government schemes. Concurrently, the District Collector subtly advises Rajeev to be "flexible" and "cooperative" for smooth administration and his own career progression, while local party workers start creating minor disruptions at the SDM office, subtly threatening non-cooperation and potential transfers.

Rajeev realizes that acceding to this demand would fundamentally compromise the integrity of public service and electoral fairness. However, outright refusal could lead to significant professional repercussions, including punitive transfers or stalling his career, and potentially create an adverse environment for his work and family.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rajeev Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
