Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ananya Sharma, a diligent Sub-Divisional Magistrate posted in the disaster-prone district of Rupnagar, was spearheading the relief operations following devastating floods. Her responsibilities included coordinating the distribution of essential supplies, managing temporary shelters, and overseeing the procurement of emergency equipment for the affected communities. The scale of the calamity demanded swift and transparent action.

During a routine audit of procurement records, Ananya uncovered discrepancies suggesting large-scale siphoning of relief funds and substandard supplies by a cartel involving local contractors and, disturbingly, her immediate superior, the District Collector, Mr. Ramesh Kumar. Mr. Kumar, a well-connected officer nearing retirement, subtly reminded her of loyalty and career implications, while the local MLA privately cautioned her against "disrupting stability" during a crisis. The affected public, already desperate, began voicing suspicions about the quality of aid.

Ananya's decision will directly impact thousands of flood victims, the integrity of the administration, and public trust in governance. Both exposing the corruption and remaining silent carry severe personal and professional consequences, potentially jeopardizing her career or perpetuating the suffering of the most vulnerable.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
