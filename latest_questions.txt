Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Amitabh Singh, a dedicated Deputy Director in the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, had recently been transferred to the Insolvency & Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) office in Mumbai. Known for his integrity, he was tasked with overseeing several high-profile insolvency cases. His current assignment involved the liquidation proceedings of a large, debt-ridden conglomerate, a case with significant public and financial implications.

During his meticulous review, Amitabh uncovered substantial financial irregularities and potential fraudulent transactions involving a prominent industrialist and certain politically connected individuals. He promptly reported his findings to the Chairman of IBBI, as per official protocol. However, instead of commendation, Amitabh soon found himself facing a series of punitive transfers, harassment through anonymous complaints, and subtle threats to his family’s safety, orchestrated by a powerful nexus attempting to silence him and bury the fraud.

Amitabh’s decision to pursue the matter further could expose powerful figures, potentially jeopardising his career and the safety of his family, but inaction would betray his oath of office, public trust, and allow large-scale corruption to continue unchecked, further eroding institutional credibility.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Amitabh Singh? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
