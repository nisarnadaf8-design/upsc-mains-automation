Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ms. Anjali Sharma is the District Welfare Officer in Palampur, a rural district grappling with significant socio-economic disparities. Known for her impeccable integrity and efficiency, she leads the Social Justice Department, responsible for verifying eligibility for various government welfare schemes, including a newly launched 'Gram Uday Yojana' aimed at poverty alleviation.

During a rapid verification drive for the 'Gram Uday Yojana', Ms. Anjali is subjected to intense pressure from Mr. Rajeev Singh, the influential local MLA, and subtly, from her immediate superior, Mr. Kumar, the District Collector. They 'advise' her to fast-track approvals for a list of beneficiaries from politically crucial villages, many of whom clearly do not meet the scheme's stringent eligibility criteria. Anjali is reminded that her career progression and future postings hinge on her 'cooperation' in ensuring the scheme's 'success' in these areas.

Ms. Anjali realizes that issuing false certificates would divert crucial public funds from genuine, needy recipients to ineligible political supporters, thereby undermining the scheme's very purpose. Her decision will have far-reaching consequences for her professional standing, the trust placed in public administration, and the welfare of the district's most vulnerable citizens.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Anjali Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
