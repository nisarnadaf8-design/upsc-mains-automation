Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Akash Kumar, a recently appointed Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) in District Rudrapur, Uttarakhand, is known for his commitment to administrative transparency and efficiency. His current responsibility includes the verification and issuance of eligibility certificates for the 'Har Gaon, Swa-Nirman' welfare scheme, designed to provide skill development grants to economically weaker sections. The upcoming by-election in the district has intensified political activity.

During a routine review, Akash receives an urgent directive from his District Collector, subtly hinting at a 'special request' from the local MLA of the ruling party. The MLA explicitly demands Akash to fast-track and approve a large batch of applications, bypassing standard verification, for individuals who clearly do not meet the scheme's income criteria, stating it would be 'beneficial for party prospects and your own career trajectory.' This would mean issuing false income certificates.

Akash's decision will directly impact the legitimate beneficiaries of the welfare scheme, the integrity of the electoral process, the public's trust in governance, and his own professional conscience. Both compliance and refusal carry significant personal and systemic consequences.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Akash Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
