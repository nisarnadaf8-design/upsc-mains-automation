Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Rahul Sharma, an IAS officer of the 2020 batch, is serving as the District Development Officer in the cyclone-ravaged coastal district of Jagatsinghpur, Odisha. Following a devastating super cyclone that left thousands homeless and displaced, he has been entrusted with overseeing critical relief and rehabilitation operations, including the procurement and distribution of essential supplies like temporary shelters, medical kits, and food grains. The central and state governments have allocated substantial emergency funds to expedite recovery.

During his extensive field visits and verification of procurement records, Rahul uncovers compelling evidence of significant irregularities and inflated billing in the purchase of relief materials. He discovers that his immediate superior, Sanjeev Kumar, IAS, the District Collector, has allegedly been colluding with a network of contractors to siphon off a considerable portion of the disaster relief funds. Sanjeev, sensing Rahul's investigation, subtly warns him about maintaining 'team cohesion' and 'not creating unnecessary hurdles' in an emergency situation, hinting at potential career repercussions. Simultaneously, a local MLA, whose relatives are linked to the beneficiary contractors, exerts veiled pressure on Rahul to drop the matter, emphasizing the need for 'swift action, not bureaucratic delays'.

The primary stakeholders affected are the vulnerable cyclone victims, whose survival and recovery depend on transparent and efficient fund utilization, and the public, whose trust in the administration is at stake. Rahul's decision will have profound consequences on his career, the integrity of the civil service, the lives of the affected population, and the accountability of public funds, regardless of whether he acts or remains silent.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rahul Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
