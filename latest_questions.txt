GS-1 SOCIETY: Globalisation and Its Impact on Indian Society
========================================

Q1. (10 Marks | 150 words)
Examine how local cultural expressions in India adapt and assert their distinctiveness amidst the homogenizing pressures of globalization, rather than being entirely subsumed. Discuss with contemporary examples that reflect a dynamic interplay between global influences and indigenous traditions.

Q2. (10 Marks | 150 words)
Critically examine how the rise of the gig economy, fueled by global digital platforms, has reshaped the traditional notions of employment and social security in urban India. Discuss the resultant socio-economic challenges for workers beyond formal labour rights.

Q3. (15 Marks | 250 words)
Globalisation has often been presented as an emancipatory force for women. Critically analyse the dual impact of globalisation on Indian women, examining both the avenues for empowerment through increased economic opportunities and autonomy, as well as the emergence of new forms of exploitation and vulnerabilities in social and economic spheres. Discuss with suitable examples.

Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Rajeev Kumar, a diligent District Project Officer in Fatehpur District, Uttar Pradesh, is responsible for overseeing the implementation and reporting of the 'Gramin Uday' welfare scheme, aimed at providing essential services to remote villages. He has just completed a comprehensive ground-level survey for the half-yearly progress report, which reveals significant discrepancies and underperformance in service delivery in several key areas.

As Rajeev prepares to submit the accurate report, he receives an urgent call from his immediate superior, the District Collector, who, under political pressure from a local MLA, instructs him to inflate the achievement figures and gloss over the deficiencies to present a more favorable picture to the State Ministry. Rajeev understands that fabricating data would secure immediate approval and prevent departmental scrutiny, but would also betray the trust of the vulnerable beneficiaries and mask systemic issues.

This decision involves multiple stakeholders including the villagers, the district administration, the political leadership, and ultimately the integrity of public service. Both compliance and defiance carry significant personal and professional consequences for Rajeev, and will profoundly impact the effectiveness of future welfare initiatives.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rajeev Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
