Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Abhishek Sharma, a young and upright Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) in the district of Rudraprayag, has been lauded for his diligent work in recent flood relief operations. His jurisdiction covers a diverse population with a history of peaceful coexistence, though underlying communal fault lines have occasionally surfaced over resource allocation. With local municipal elections just three months away, the political climate is increasingly charged.

A minor dispute over the usage of a public ground, used for religious processions by one community and as a weekly market by another, escalates into widespread communal tension following inflammatory speeches by local political leaders. As SDM, Abhishek is tasked with restoring order. His immediate superior, the District Magistrate, has instructed him to maintain absolute neutrality and enforce the rule of law without prejudice. However, the powerful local MLA, whose party relies heavily on the vote bank of one of the communities, directly contacts Abhishek. The MLA explicitly demands that Abhishek take biased action by arresting key individuals from the opposing community and imposing restrictions that disproportionately affect them, ensuring a 'favourable' outcome for the upcoming elections. The MLA subtly reminds Abhishek of potential career repercussions if his 'requests' are not met.

The situation on the ground is volatile, with social media amplifying rumours and a large crowd gathering, ready to react to the administration's decision. Abhishek knows that any biased action will permanently damage his credibility and the public’s trust in the administration, while defying the MLA might lead to political reprisal and potentially prolonged agitation if the MLA instigates further.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Abhishek Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved from the political master while upholding your duties? (150 words)
