Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Rohan Sharma, a bright young IAS officer, was recently posted as Sub-Divisional Magistrate in the bustling industrial district of Rudrapur, Uttarakhand. Enthusiastic about public service, he began his tenure by meticulously reviewing major ongoing infrastructure projects, particularly those involving large-scale land acquisitions for urban development.

During his review, Rohan uncovered glaring irregularities in the acquisition process for a proposed 'Smart City Township' project, primarily benefiting a consortium of private builders with political connections. Agricultural lands were undervalued, environmental clearances appeared fast-tracked without proper due diligence, and resettlement plans for affected farmers were vague. His immediate superior, the District Collector, subtly advised him to expedite pending clearances, citing "urgent state interest." Simultaneously, a powerful local politician publicly lauded the project, cautioning against any 'bureaucratic hurdles'.

Rohan is torn between his duty to uphold the law and the immense pressures to conform. Farmers stand to lose their ancestral lands and livelihoods without fair compensation, while the public stands to suffer from environmental degradation and a loss of public trust in governance. Any action he takes could jeopardize his nascent career, but inaction would make him complicit in a potentially large-scale scam.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rohan Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
