Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Rajesh Kumar, a highly respected District Collector in Palghat District, Kerala, is known for his unwavering integrity and efficiency, particularly in mediating complex land dispute cases. His office is currently overseeing the expedited processing of land records to facilitate a critical infrastructure project, requiring meticulous scrutiny and a transparent approach to all pending disputes.

A significant land dispute case, involving a large tract of agricultural land vital for this public infrastructure, lands on Rajesh’s desk for final review and decision. To his profound dismay, he discovers that his estranged brother, Suresh, has a direct financial interest in one of the disputing parties, a prominent private real estate firm. Suresh approaches Rajesh privately, hinting at potential family discord and public embarrassment if the decision doesn't favor his associate, while a local political leader also subtly pressures him to expedite a favorable outcome for the real estate firm.

The farmers whose livelihoods depend on this land, the real estate firm with its commercial interests, the critical infrastructure project's timeline, the public's trust in impartial governance, and Rajesh's personal family relationships are all at stake. Both a biased decision and perceived evasion of duty carry serious, far-reaching consequences.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
The case presents a clear conflict of interest for Rajesh, compromising his impartiality and integrity. It involves potential nepotism, a threat to public trust, and raises questions of accountability versus familial loyalty. Political pressure further complicates adherence to ethical governance principles and constitutional morality.

(b) What are the options available to Rajesh Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
Option 1: Recuse himself from the case. Merits: Upholds impartiality, avoids conflict of interest. Demerits: Might delay project, seen as evading responsibility, superior might resist.
Option 2: Decide strictly on merit. Merits: Upholds integrity, ensures justice. Demerits: Family discord, political backlash, potential personal harassment.
Option 3: Seek guidance from his superior. Merits: Shares responsibility, follows protocol. Demerits: Might be seen as indecisiveness, superior could also face pressure.

(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
I would recommend Rajesh immediately declare the conflict of interest to his superior in writing, formally recusing himself from this specific case. He should request its reassignment to another competent officer or a higher authority for an unbiased review, citing the principles of integrity and fair play. This course upholds impartiality, safeguards public trust, and aligns with service rules.
To handle pressures, Rajesh should firmly but politely inform his brother about his ethical obligations and legal ramifications, reiterating his commitment to justice over personal ties. He must document the political pressure and report it to appropriate channels, emphasizing the need for procedural fairness for the infrastructure project's long-term success. Transparency about his recusal will also deter speculation.
