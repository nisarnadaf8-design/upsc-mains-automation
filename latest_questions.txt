Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Rajesh Kumar is the District Magistrate of Kolar district, known for his integrity and proactive administrative approach. He is currently overseeing a crucial multi-departmental investigation into a major land encroachment scandal involving several prominent local figures and a large tract of forest land. This investigation has garnered significant public attention and political scrutiny due to its sensitive nature.

During the ongoing investigation, Rajesh discovers that a distant cousin, who is part of a large joint family, is implicated in one of the land encroachment cases within his jurisdiction. The cousin, aware of Rajesh's position, discreetly approaches him through a common family acquaintance, requesting leniency and suggesting that publicizing his involvement might bring disrepute to the entire family. Simultaneously, the Chief Minister's office has conveyed an urgent directive to expedite the investigation and ensure that no individual, however influential, is spared, given the high-profile nature of the case.

The local media is closely following every development, and public trust in the administration’s ability to act impartially is at stake. Rajesh’s decision will not only impact his career but also set a precedent for future handling of similar high-stakes cases in the district.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rajesh Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
