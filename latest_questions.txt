Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Divya Sharma, a newly posted District Magistrate in Rudrapur district, Uttarakhand, is known for her meticulous and proactive approach to public administration, especially in enforcing social welfare laws. On a routine inspection tour, responding to community intelligence, she visited the premises of 'Shakti Garments', a prominent textile factory that is a major employer in the region.

During her inspection, Ms. Sharma's team uncovered irrefutable evidence of systematic child labour involving several underage children working in hazardous conditions. It was subsequently revealed that 'Shakti Garments' is indirectly owned by Mr. Suryakant Tiwari, a highly influential Member of Parliament from the ruling party, known for his political patronage in the state. Ms. Sharma's immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, has discreetly advised 'exercising caution and discretion', implying a delay in formal proceedings. Further, subtle but clear threats of adverse career implications have been conveyed to her through various channels if she proceeds with strict legal action.

Ms. Sharma now faces a profound ethical conflict. Her decision will profoundly affect the exploited children, the factory's future, the public's trust in the administration, and her own professional integrity and career trajectory. Both swift, decisive action and any perceived hesitation carry significant and far-reaching consequences for all stakeholders.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Divya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
