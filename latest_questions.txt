GS-1 SOCIETY: Tribal Communities: Issues, Rights and Constitutional Safeguards
========================================

Q1. (10 Marks | 150 words)
Examine the inherent limitations of the Fifth and Sixth Schedule provisions in addressing the evolving demands for greater autonomy and self-determination among various tribal communities in resource-rich regions, despite their foundational intent.

Q2. (10 Marks | 150 words)
What do you understand by the concept of "development-induced precarity" experienced by tribal communities, and how has the implementation of the Forest Rights Act, 2006, sometimes fallen short in mitigating this precariousness, especially in critical mineral exploitation zones?

Q3. (15 Marks | 250 words)
Critically analyze how the interplay of resource exploitation, land alienation, and the slow pace of implementing constitutional safeguards exacerbates the socio-economic and political marginalisation of tribal communities, often leading to deep-seated grievances. Discuss the implications for maintaining their distinct cultural identity and preventing further radicalization.

========================================

Q4. (10 Marks | 150 words)

Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Mr. Alok Verma, a Deputy Secretary in the Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance, New Delhi, recently uncovered significant financial irregularities and kickbacks amounting to several hundred crores in a large national infrastructure project. He formally reported this detailed information, with irrefutable evidence, to the Central Vigilance Commission (CVC).

Upon reporting, Alok has been immediately transferred to a remote, non-critical post in a distant state, his performance appraisals are being reviewed negatively, and his family is receiving anonymous threats, creating immense personal stress. His superior, the Joint Secretary, informally advised him to withdraw his complaint for the sake of his career and family’s safety, while a local political leader indirectly warned him against tarnishing the project’s reputation and causing public panic.

Alok now faces immense pressure from all sides. If he continues, he fears severe career damage and real threats to his family. If he retracts, public funds remain siphoned, the corrupt go unpunished, and his own integrity is compromised, eroding public trust in the system.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Mr. Alok Verma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
