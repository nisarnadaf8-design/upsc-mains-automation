Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Officer Arjun Sharma, a diligent and upright District Disaster Management Officer in Rudraprayag District, Uttarakhand, is currently overseeing critical relief operations following unprecedented monsoon floods. The disaster has devastated numerous villages, displaced thousands, and led to a severe humanitarian crisis requiring immediate provision of food, shelter, and medical supplies from the allocated substantial relief funds.

Amidst the chaos and urgent need, Arjun faces immense pressure. The District Collector, his immediate superior, hints at a 'political directive' to divert a significant portion of the allocated flood relief funds towards a long-pending, non-emergency infrastructure project in a politically sensitive constituency, citing 'future flood prevention' as a pretext. Simultaneously, a powerful local MLA directly pressures Arjun, suggesting that his career progression hinges on his compliance with this diversion. Arjun knows that any diversion will directly jeopardize the lives and recovery of the most vulnerable flood victims.

The lives of thousands of flood-affected citizens hang in the balance, dependent on these relief funds. Arjun's decision will not only impact their immediate survival and rehabilitation but also the credibility of the district administration and the public's trust in governance. Both compliance and defiance carry severe personal and professional consequences for him.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Arjun Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
