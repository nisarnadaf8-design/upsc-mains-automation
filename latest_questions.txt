Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Rajesh Kumar, a diligent and upright District Collector in the resource-rich district of Kolar, is known for his commitment to transparent governance. His current responsibilities include overseeing various infrastructure projects, particularly those related to renewable energy and land acquisition for industrial development, ensuring compliance with environmental norms and fair compensation.

A major solar park project, crucial for the state's energy goals, is under review in Rajesh's office for final environmental clearances and land valuation approvals. Allegations have surfaced from local villagers and NGOs regarding irregularities in the land acquisition process and inadequate environmental impact assessments. To Rajesh's dismay, he discovers that a key contractor company, 'Sunbeam Renewables', involved in the project, is owned by his brother-in-law, Jitendra Singh. Jitendra contacts Rajesh directly, appealing for swift approval, citing project deadlines and significant financial implications, while a prominent local political leader also pressures Rajesh to expedite the clearances for "developmental progress". Simultaneously, reports of intensified protests by affected farmers demanding a re-evaluation of land compensation are reaching his office.

Rajesh's decision will directly affect his professional integrity, his family relationships, the welfare of hundreds of villagers, the project's viability, the credibility of the district administration, and potentially lead to legal challenges. Both an immediate approval and a prolonged investigation carry severe personal and institutional consequences.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case.
(b) What are the options available to Rajesh Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits.
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved?
