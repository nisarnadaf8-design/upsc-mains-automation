Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Ritu Sharma, an upright and dedicated District Magistrate of Ramnagar district, has recently been lauded for her efforts in streamlining public service delivery and enforcing environmental regulations. Her department is currently overseeing a critical land acquisition process for a new industrial corridor, vital for regional economic growth and job creation.

A powerful State Minister, Mr. Surendra Singh, personally contacts Ritu, insisting she fast-track the acquisition of a specific parcel of land, currently a small wetland crucial for local biodiversity and inhabited by a tribal community, to favour a private developer known to be his close associate. He subtly hints at adverse career consequences, including a possible transfer to an obscure posting, if she fails to "understand the larger political imperatives." Her immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, also advises her informally to be "pragmatic" to avoid friction and ensure the project's smooth progress.

The decision will directly impact the tribal community's livelihood and cultural heritage, the delicate local ecosystem, and the public's perception of administrative fairness. Upholding the rules might lead to political reprisal against Ritu and potential derailment of her career, while bending them would compromise environmental protection, social justice, and the integrity of public service, potentially inviting public outcry and legal challenges.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case.
(b) What are the options available to Ritu Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits.
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved from the Minister and your superior?
