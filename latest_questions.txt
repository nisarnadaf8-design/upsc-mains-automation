Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Rakesh Kumar, a diligent Superintendent of Police in Bilaspur district, has built a reputation for integrity and impartiality over his 15 years of service. His district is ecologically sensitive and has a significant tribal population, for whom land rights are a contentious issue often leading to socio-legal disputes.

Recently, a case of alleged illegal encroachment on communally owned tribal land for a large-scale commercial poultry farm has been reported. The investigation falls directly under Rakesh's jurisdiction. To his dismay, he discovers that his younger brother, Manoj Kumar, is the principal investor and alleged perpetrator of the encroachment. His parents have appealed to him emotionally to protect Manoj, while the local MLA, a close acquaintance of his brother, has subtly suggested expediting the investigation in Manoj's favor.

The tribal community has launched protests, demanding immediate and fair action, garnering significant media attention. His superior officer expects a transparent and swift resolution. Any decision Rakesh makes will have profound implications not only for his family and career but also for the trust placed in the police force by vulnerable communities and the rule of law itself.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rakesh Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
