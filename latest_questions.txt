Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Officer Akash Verma, Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) in the Anti-Corruption Bureau (ACB) of Indrapuri State, is a decorated officer known for his impeccable integrity. Recently transferred to head a critical investigation, he has been tasked with unearthing a widespread land acquisition scam involving nexus between real estate developers and officials in the Urban Development Department, leading to significant financial losses for the state exchequer and displacement of local farmers.

During the exhaustive investigation, Akash uncovers irrefutable evidence implicating a highly influential, recently retired Additional Director General of Police (ADGP) in receiving substantial kickbacks related to the same land deals. This retired officer, who served in a key administrative role within the state police department (not ACB) at the time, was a former mentor to Akash and enjoys significant clout with the current Director General of Police (DGP). Exposing this senior police involvement would not only bring severe disrepute to the entire state police force but could also raise questions about the ACB's past oversight, potentially destabilizing the institution and inviting intense political scrutiny. His immediate superior, the ACB Director, has informally counselled him to prioritize "maintaining institutional stability" and avoid "opening a Pandora's box" that could jeopardize ongoing reforms.

The victims of the land scam, the general public's trust in governance, the integrity of the police department, Akash's career, the reputation of his superior and the DGP, and the broader political landscape of the state are all critically affected by his decision. Both proceeding with the full truth and selectively prosecuting carry profound and far-reaching consequences.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Akash Verma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
