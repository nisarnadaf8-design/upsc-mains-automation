Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
SP Avinash Sharma, a newly transferred officer renowned for his impeccable integrity, has taken charge of the Anti-Corruption Unit (ACU) in the bustling metropolis of Indraprastha. His department has been under intense public scrutiny due to a history of low conviction rates and allegations of political interference in high-profile cases. The public’s trust in the police force is at an all-time low, with widespread perception of deep-seated corruption.

During a routine, yet thorough, review of past investigations and internal audit reports, Avinash uncovers irrefutable evidence pointing to a meticulously organized bribery and protection racket operating within the city's Traffic Department. The evidence strongly implicates several senior officers, including a few who had been his mentors during his early career, and indicates possible complicity extending to higher echelons, potentially even reaching the Police Commissioner's office. His immediate superior, who has always championed his career, subtly advises extreme caution, citing the potential for departmental disrepute and the adverse impact on his promising career. Concurrently, an influential political leader's office discreetly conveys a message suggesting that the matter be handled "internally and with utmost discretion" to avoid public panic and political embarrassment.

Exposing the scam would undoubtedly lead to massive departmental upheaval, a potential public relations nightmare, and significant personal risk to Avinash's career. However, suppressing or downplaying the findings would gravely undermine the rule of law, erode the already fragile public trust, and compromise his personal integrity and the credibility of the ACU.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to SP Avinash Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
