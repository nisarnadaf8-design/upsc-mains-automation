Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Ms. Aparna Singh, a diligent Deputy Secretary in the Ministry of Defence, based in New Delhi, is tasked with overseeing procurement files for critical defence equipment. Her department is currently under public scrutiny regarding certain past acquisitions, particularly a deal signed five years ago for a advanced missile defence system.

An RTI application from a renowned investigative journalist seeks detailed financial records, internal audit reports, and correspondence related to this high-profile missile system acquisition, alleging significant cost overruns and procedural irregularities. Releasing these specific details could potentially expose sensitive national security information, compromise ongoing diplomatic relations with the supplier nation, and jeopardize future strategic partnerships. Her immediate superior, the Joint Secretary, has informally but firmly advised her to withhold certain portions of the information citing 'national security implications' and 'strategic confidentiality', while a prominent opposition leader has publicly called for full transparency, threatening legal action and public protests if the information is denied.

The decision Ms. Singh makes will directly impact the RTI applicant's quest for information, the Ministry's reputation for integrity, national security interests, and potentially her own career prospects. Both full disclosure and complete withholding of information carry significant risks and potential backlash from different quarters.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Aparna Singh? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
