Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Rajesh Kumar is the District Disaster Management Officer in the flood-prone district of Mahanagar. Following unprecedented monsoon rains, the district has been severely hit by floods, displacing thousands and causing widespread destruction. He has been tasked with overseeing the distribution of critical relief funds and materials to the affected population. His reputation for honesty and efficiency is well-known.

As Rajesh mobilizes resources, he receives a direct order from the powerful local MLA, Mr. Sharma, to divert a significant portion of the allocated relief funds towards a specific 'reconstruction project' in his own constituency, which is less severely affected. Simultaneously, Rajesh's immediate superior, the District Collector, hints that cooperation with influential political figures is crucial for 'smooth functioning' and future career prospects, indirectly endorsing the MLA's demand. The local media has also begun questioning the transparency of the relief operations, increasing public scrutiny.

The primary stakeholders are the genuinely flood-affected populace, who desperately need aid for survival and rehabilitation. Diverting funds would directly deprive them of essential support, leading to potential loss of life, prolonged suffering, and social unrest. Refusing the MLA and Collector could jeopardize Rajesh's career and potentially lead to his transfer or ostracization, hindering future public service effectiveness.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rajesh Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
