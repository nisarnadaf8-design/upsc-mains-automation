Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Paragraph 1 — Background and Context (3-4 lines):
Ms. Anjali Sharma, a young and dynamic District Magistrate of Palamuru district in Telangana, is overseeing the implementation of the ambitious 'SagarMala Inland Waterways Project'. This national project aims to connect crucial industrial hubs, promising significant economic growth and employment generation for the region. The first phase involves acquiring land, including a large tract designated as sacred forest land by the indigenous Gond tribal community of village Rampur, a PESA-notified area.

Paragraph 2 — The Dilemma (3-4 lines):
The project's completion is a national priority, with the Chief Minister personally monitoring its progress. Your immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, has urged you to expedite land acquisition, emphasizing that any delay would lead to cost overruns and political backlash. However, the Gond community vehemently opposes the acquisition of their ancestral sacred groves, citing religious sentiments, cultural identity, and traditional forest rights under the Forest Rights Act, 2006. Local tribal leaders have organised peaceful protests, gaining significant media attention and sympathy from human rights activists. Simultaneously, a powerful industrial lobby group, keen on the project's early completion, is exerting pressure on your office, promising investments and development.

Paragraph 3 — Stakeholders and Consequences (2-3 lines):
Your decision will directly impact the economic development of the state, the cultural and religious identity of the Gond community, the credibility of the government, and your own career. Acquiescence to the project could mean the irreversible loss of an ancient cultural heritage and displacement of vulnerable communities, while resisting could lead to project delays, economic setbacks, and potential disciplinary action against you.

Sub-questions (exactly as UPSC format):
(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Anjali Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
