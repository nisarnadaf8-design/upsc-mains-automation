Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ms. Ananya Sharma, a diligent District Collector in District X, a remote tribal-dominated region, faces a critical situation. The 'National Green Energy Corridor' project, a flagship national infrastructure initiative, requires a vital segment of its high-tension transmission line to pass directly through a revered ancestral forest area. This forest is central to the cultural and spiritual identity and livelihood of the indigenous 'Vanvasi' community, who lack formal land titles but have traditionally resided there for centuries. The project promises significant national energy security and regional development.

Ananya is under immense pressure from the Chief Secretary to ensure swift land acquisition and project completion to avoid substantial cost overruns and political scrutiny at the national level. Simultaneously, a powerful local politician, with undisclosed business interests linked to the project's contractors, is subtly inciting local factions against the Vanvasi community while publicly demanding adherence to national development goals. The Vanvasi community, led by an articulate elder, vehemently opposes displacement, asserting their unrecognised community forest rights under the Forest Rights Act and highlighting the ecological fragility of the area. Public sentiment is divided, with environmental activists supporting the tribal community and urban stakeholders prioritising energy infrastructure.

Ananya's decision will directly impact the Vanvasi community's socio-cultural survival, their trust in the administration, and fundamental human rights. Conversely, any significant delay could jeopardise a nationally critical project, lead to severe financial penalties for the government, and invite political repercussions, potentially affecting her career.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ms. Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
