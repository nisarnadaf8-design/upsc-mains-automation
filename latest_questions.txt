Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Manoj Kumar is the diligent Project Director for the 'Gram Samridhi Yojana' under the Ministry of Rural Development, overseeing its implementation in a socio-economically disadvantaged district in eastern Uttar Pradesh. The scheme aims to provide crucial skill development and livelihood support to the rural poor. Manoj’s team has just completed rigorous ground surveys for the quarterly performance report, which is vital for future funding allocations and policy evaluations.

The raw data, however, reveals a significant shortfall in achieving set targets and a lower-than-anticipated effective beneficiary outreach, reflecting genuine implementation challenges. His immediate superior, the Joint Secretary, upon reviewing the preliminary findings, expresses "deep disappointment" and implicitly suggests "adjusting" beneficiary numbers and success rates upwards to ensure continued funding and avoid negative political fallout, especially with state elections approaching. Simultaneously, the local Member of Parliament (MP) pressures Manoj, insisting that a "positive report" is imperative to maintain public confidence in the government's welfare initiatives. Manoj finds himself in a severe ethical bind, caught between presenting accurate, albeit unfavorable, data and succumbing to pressure to manipulate figures to safeguard his career and potentially secure further funds for a scheme that does benefit some, albeit not as widely as hoped.

Manoj’s decision will profoundly impact the genuine beneficiaries, the integrity of government data and policy-making, the credibility of his department, and his own professional conscience. Both adhering to the truth or yielding to pressure carry serious, far-reaching consequences for the scheme's future and his personal integrity.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Manoj Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
