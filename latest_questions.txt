Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Mr. Alok Verma, District Collector of Bastar district in Chhattisgarh, is overseeing a critical infrastructure project. The State Government has approved a large-scale bauxite mining venture, touted as vital for economic growth and employment generation, slated to commence in a region traditionally inhabited by the particularly vulnerable Pondo tribal community, whose livelihoods are intrinsically linked to the forest ecosystem.

Mr. Verma receives explicit directives from the Chief Secretary to expedite land acquisition and displacement procedures, ensuring no delays to the mining project. Concurrently, tribal elders and local community activists present him with compelling evidence of grave environmental damage, cultural disruption, and inadequate rehabilitation plans that severely undermine their ancestral rights and traditional way of life, leading to widespread protests. The local MLA also pressures him to demonstrate visible progress for state-level political gains.

His decision will directly impact the survival and cultural integrity of the Pondo tribal community, the economic ambitions of the state and the mining company, and the region's delicate ecological balance. Both forceful implementation and outright defiance carry significant social, political, and personal ramifications for Mr. Verma and his administration.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Mr. Alok Verma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
