Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Rajesh Kumar, a young and diligent District Magistrate in the industrially developing district of Shanti Nagar, initiated a comprehensive drive against illegal labour practices. During a surprise inspection at a large garment factory, 'Prosperity Apparel', situated in a secluded zone, his team unearthed concrete evidence of child labour, with several minors working in unhygienic and unsafe conditions. The factory is widely known to be owned by Mr. Rakesh Verma, a powerful State Cabinet Minister with significant political clout and a reputation for ruthlessness.

Upon discovery, the factory manager immediately offered Rajesh a substantial financial inducement to overlook the findings and threatened severe political repercussions, including transfer and potential career stagnation, if he proceeded with official action. Subsequently, Rajesh received a discreet phone call from his immediate superior, the Divisional Commissioner, who, without directly instructing, advised 'prudence' and 'political sensitivity' in handling the matter, hinting at the minister's influence reaching the highest echelons. Rajesh is aware that pursuing the case vigorously could jeopardize his budding career and face institutional pushback, while inaction would mean a grave compromise of his ethical principles and a betrayal of his oath.

The exploited children and their impoverished families, the factory workers whose livelihoods might be affected, the minister's political standing, the credibility of the district administration, and Rajesh's personal integrity are all at stake. Both decisive action and passive oversight carry far-reaching consequences.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rajesh Kumar? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
