Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Rohan Verma, a diligent District Planning Officer in a rural district of Uttar Pradesh, was tasked with compiling the final impact assessment report for the flagship 'Gramin Jan Kalyan Yojana' – a major government welfare scheme aimed at poverty alleviation and infrastructure development over the past five years. His team had conducted extensive fieldwork, collecting data directly from beneficiaries and local administrative units, revealing several critical gaps and underperformance issues.

As the deadline approached, Rohan's immediate superior, the Additional District Magistrate, along with a powerful local MLA, exerted immense pressure on him to significantly inflate the reported beneficiary numbers and project completion rates in the final report. They argued that positive figures were crucial for securing further central grants and enhancing the district's image, implying severe career repercussions if he failed to comply and suggesting the report would be altered regardless.

The manipulation of data would directly benefit political figures and some officials, but would gravely mislead policymakers, divert resources from genuine needs, undermine public trust in governance, and ultimately harm the very rural populace the scheme was meant to serve. Both compliance and resistance carry significant professional and personal consequences for Rohan.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rohan Verma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
