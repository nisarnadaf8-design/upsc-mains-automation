Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY
Ananya Sharma, a newly posted District Magistrate in a flood-ravaged district of Odisha, has been tirelessly leading relief and rehabilitation operations. The district, already vulnerable, experienced unprecedented damage, leading to a critical need for rapid and transparent distribution of aid. Her senior, the Divisional Commissioner, Mr. Rakesh Verma, has been overseeing the procurement of essential supplies for the entire division.

During a routine audit of procurement records for emergency medical supplies and temporary shelters, Ananya uncovers irrefutable evidence of gross financial irregularities, indicating large-scale embezzlement involving inflated prices and substandard materials. The evidence strongly implicates Mr. Verma, who is known to be well-connected politically and is also her direct reporting authority. A local political leader, whose contractor firm benefited, subtly warns Ananya about potential career repercussions if she pursues the matter, citing the need for 'unity' during the crisis.

Her decision will directly impact thousands of desperate flood victims whose lives depend on adequate aid, the integrity of the administration, and the public's faith in the system. Both exposing the corruption and remaining silent carry significant personal and professional risks for Ananya.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Ananya Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
