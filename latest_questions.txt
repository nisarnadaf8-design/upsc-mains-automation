Q6. (20 Marks | 300 words) — CASE STUDY

Rohan Sharma, a diligent District Environmental Officer recently posted to the bustling industrial district of Siddharthnagar, is keen on ensuring strict adherence to environmental norms. His department, the State Pollution Control Board, is responsible for monitoring industrial compliance and safeguarding ecological balance in the region. Siddharthnagar is home to several large manufacturing units, including 'GreenOzone Industries', a significant employer and revenue generator for the state.

During a routine inspection, Rohan's team uncovered serious violations by GreenOzone Industries, particularly concerning untreated effluent discharge into a nearby river, impacting local farming communities. Before he could initiate punitive action, his immediate superior, a senior bureaucrat, subtly advised him to 'handle the matter sensitively,' hinting at the industry's economic clout and political connections. Concurrently, the local Member of Legislative Assembly (MLA) contacted Rohan, emphasizing the company's contribution to employment and warning against any action that might deter investment or cause job losses.

Rohan's decision will directly affect the health and livelihood of downstream communities, the ecological integrity of the river system, the reputation of his department, and the state's industrial policy. Both strict enforcement and overlooking the violations carry significant professional and ethical ramifications for him.

(a) Identify and discuss the ethical issues involved in this case. (50 words)
(b) What are the options available to Rohan Sharma? Evaluate each option with its merits and demerits. (100 words)
(c) What course of action would you recommend and why? How would you handle the pressures involved? (150 words)
